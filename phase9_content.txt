**Sprint:** 9.1 — Operational Governance: First Constitutional Extension
**Phase:** 9 — Operational Governance
**Nature:** Constitutional design exercise — declarative only
**Status:** PROPOSED (pending independent constitutional review)
**Date:** August 2026
**Baseline:** Constitution v1.0 (permanently frozen); Phases 1–8; BOE; Deployment
Runtime; Research Extension; Identity Boundary (Sprint 8.4)

> This is a constitutional design exercise. It is not an implementation plan,
> not an architecture redesign, and not a governance implementation. It answers
> one question only: **what is the smallest constitutional extension required to
> begin Operational Governance under Constitution v1.0?**

---

## 1. Constitutional Mission

> **Operational Governance records the immutable, human-authorized linkage
> between a validated StrategyManifest and its runtime deployment identity, so
> that deployment clearance is a permanent, auditable architectural fact — and
> nothing else.**

This mission does not overlap any frozen phase:

- **Phase 4 (Capital Allocation):** governance records clearance, not capital,
  exposure, weights, or portfolio composition.
- **Phase 5 (Execution):** governance places no orders, opens no connections,
  and translates no broker commands.
- **Phase 6 (Scientific Validation):** governance does not judge scientific
  truth; authorization is operational, not epistemological.
- **Phase 7 (Deployment Runtime):** governance does not run, pause, resume, or
  orchestrate the runtime; it records a decision that others may observe.
- **Phase 8 (Research Industrialization):** governance does not evaluate
  outcomes, feed research, or interpret evidence; it sits outside the research
  stream.

## 3. Constitutional Outputs

Phase 9 introduces exactly **one** new permanent operational fact:

**The Operational Authorization** — the immutable record that a specific,
validated `StrategyManifest` was operationally cleared by an identified human
operator for a specific runtime deployment identity, at a recorded time.

This is the fact the Identity Boundary (Sprint 8.4) explicitly assigned to
governance: *"this manifest was cleared to deploy as this runtime identity."*
No frozen phase owns it today; no frozen object can produce it; and Constitution
v1.0 Principle 15 requires it to have exactly one owner. It is the only
architectural fact whose owner is currently unassigned, and therefore the only
fact Phase 9 is constitutionally obligated to introduce.

Because the Operational Authorization is a **permanent operational fact** —
immutable, attributable, timestamped, auditable, referenced, and persistent —
it cannot remain purely declarative. A permanent fact of this kind requires the
**minimum persistent architectural representation** that makes it real and
referable. The form of that representation is defined in §6.

No additional facts are introduced. No second fact is proposed: any additional
fact (status, ranking, judgement, recommendation) is either already owned by a
frozen phase or is constitutionally forbidden (§4).

## 6. Minimal Architecture

The constitutional fact defined in §3 is permanent. It must therefore possess a
minimum persistent architectural representation. Constitutional minimality means
the smallest representation that makes the fact real, attributable, timestamped,
auditable, referenced, and persistent — never less (under-representation), never
more (expansion).

### Objects

Exactly **ONE** immutable domain object:

**`OperationalAuthorization`**

This object exists only to represent the single constitutional operational
fact. It contains:

- No scientific reasoning.
- No runtime behaviour.
- No lifecycle.
- No execution logic.
- No recommendations.
- No optimization.
- No portfolio logic.

It is only the immutable authorization record.

### Services

Exactly **TWO** stateless services:

1. One **append service** — records a new `OperationalAuthorization`.
2. One **read-only service** — returns `OperationalAuthorization` records.

This mirrors the already frozen Phase 8 write/read separation
(`OutcomeAppender` / `OutcomeReader`). The Phase 8 pattern is not redesigned; it
is reused constitutionally. A single combined read-write service is rejected
because the frozen write/read split is the canonical separation.

### Repository

Exactly **ONE** immutable repository:

- Persists `OperationalAuthorization` records.
- No updates.
- No deletion.
- Append-only.
- Read-only observation.

Persistence is required because the constitutional fact itself is permanent: a
permanent, attributable, timestamped, auditable, referenced fact must have a
durable home, or it ceases to be auditable.

### Architectural Inventory

```
1 immutable domain object
2 stateless services
1 immutable repository
```

This is the **constitutional minimum**, not architectural expansion:

- The object is the minimal representation of the single fact.
- The two services are the minimal realization of the frozen write/read
  discipline applied to that one fact.
- The repository is the minimal durable home the permanent fact requires.

Nothing else is added: no further objects, no runtime components, no
orchestration, no automation.

**Why this is minimal, not expansion:** Sprint 8.4 resolved ownership of an
*existing* architectural boundary and therefore required no software
representation. Sprint 9.1 introduces a *new permanent operational fact*.
Unlike a constitutional boundary, a permanent operational fact requires a
minimal architectural representation. This distinction justifies
1 object · 2 services · 1 repository without violating constitutional
minimality — the representation is exactly the smallest one that makes the fact
permanent, and there is exactly one representation per permanent fact.

## 7. Boundary Analysis

Phase 9 cannot influence any frozen concern. Each is stated explicitly:

- **Research:** governance records deployment clearance; it never declares,
  modifies, or terminates research.
- **Validation:** governance never assesses, approves, or overturns scientific
  validity; that authority belongs solely to Phase 6 and Human Research Review.
- **Execution:** governance has no path to orders, brokers, or transports.
- **Deployment Runtime:** governance issues no commands; it does not start,
  stop, pause, or resume; lifecycle state remains exclusively Phase 7's.
- **Evidence:** governance reads evidence as an observer; it never creates,
  appends to, or deletes the scientific evidence set (Principle 20).
- **OutcomeReader:** governance consumes through the frozen read-only reader; it
  introduces no parallel consumption path.
- **Human Research Review:** governance operates on the operational side; it
  never substitutes for or modifies the human scientific authority, which
  resides permanently outside software.

Because Phase 9 introduces only the constitutional minimum — one immutable
authorization object, one append-only service, one read-only service, and one
immutable repository — and because these components exist solely to represent
the permanent Operational Authorization fact, they introduce no authority over
research, validation, runtime, execution, lifecycle, or scientific evidence.
Therefore no frozen constitutional boundary is altered.

## 8. Future Growth

Sprint 9.1 establishes the `OperationalAuthorization` foundation. Later
Sprint 9.x work begins from this foundation and extends it. Each is identified
as a natural extension; none is designed here.

- **Sprint 9.2 — Operational authorization workflow:** the operational process
  by which authorizations are granted and recorded, built on the immutable
  `OperationalAuthorization` foundation.
- **Sprint 9.3 — Operational audit:** auditability of the authorization record —
  demonstrating the append-only record is complete and unalterable.
- **Sprint 9.4 — Deployment governance:** governance decisions that consume
  authorization records as their basis.
- **Later — Production tooling, monitoring, visualization, reporting:** product
  surface concerns that render the authorization record observable to operators.

All of these extend the `OperationalAuthorization` foundation. None of them
introduces a new permanent fact; each reuses the single fact and its append /
read paths. Each must independently justify itself as the smallest possible
extension at the time it is proposed, and each must preserve the boundaries of
§7.

## 9. Final Recommendation

**Sprint 9.1 introduces ONLY:**

1. **`OperationalAuthorization`** — one immutable domain object representing
   the single permanent operational fact (§3).
2. **Append path** — one stateless append service.
3. **Read path** — one stateless read-only service.
4. **Immutable repository** — one durable, append-only store for the fact.

Nothing else is introduced.

Sprint 9.1 explicitly rejects: portfolio management, capital allocation, runtime
orchestration, deployment lifecycle, status transitions, strategy ranking,
scientific interpretation, execution logic, optimization, and automation. Each
of these belongs either to a frozen phase (capital → Phase 4, execution →
Phase 5, validation → Phase 6, lifecycle → Phase 7, research → Phase 8 and
Human Research Review) or to a future governance extension that must
independently justify itself (§8).

The result is the smallest constitutional extension possible while ensuring that
every permanent operational fact has exactly one persistent architectural
representation.
