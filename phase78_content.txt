## 5. Remaining Known Debt

### Accepted Technical Debt
- **`DecisionPolicy.evaluate()` Signature Mismatch:** The Orchestrator calls the decision policy without passing a `timestamp`, but the contract requires one. This will cause a `TypeError` at runtime with concrete implementations.
- **`PaperTradingAccount` Mutability:** The paper trading simulator uses mutable `Dict` and `List` fields for internal state. While appropriate for a simulator, it is a technical exception to the strict immutability convention.
- **`ExecutionEngineContract.execute()` Type Safety:** The execution contract returns `Any` rather than a strictly typed `ExecutionResult` or a Future of it, weakening static type analysis.

### Future Enhancements
- **Multi-Snapshot Orchestration:** The current `DeploymentOrchestrator` is stateless and processes each snapshot in total isolation (instantiating a new Candidate and BOW per snapshot). It cannot observe phenomena *across* snapshots (such as recoil over multiple candles). The orchestrator will need stateful extensions to handle temporal evidence.
- **Exit Mechanism:** The pipeline is currently unidirectional toward opening positions. There is no orchestrator mechanism to monitor an open position and route an exit `PositionSpecification`.

### Out of Scope
- **Concrete Detector Implementation:** The `BehaviorDetectorContract` remains unimplemented. Implementing the validated `MeanReversionDetector` is the objective of the next phase.

## 2. Scope

**Paper Trading Runner** — The execution flow coordinator that drives market observations through the orchestrator and forwards resulting position specifications to the execution layer, encapsulating per-snapshot failures in deterministic outcomes.

**Simulation Execution Integration** — The contract-level integration with the Execution Domain ensuring deterministic, broker-independent simulation of position specifications into execution outcomes.

Nothing else.

The following principles are permanent rules for the Deployment Layer:

**Deployment owns orchestration.**

**Deployment owns construction.**  
The bootstrap, assembler, and runtime configuration objects construct the deployment graph. They do not execute it.

**Deployment never owns science.**  
No observation, evidence, behaviour, interpretation, decision, or validation logic exists in the Deployment Layer. Scientific reasoning is consumed, never produced.

**Deployment never owns execution physics.**  
No broker communication, order routing, fill simulation, or position tracking exists in the Deployment Layer. Execution is delegated to the Execution Domain.

**Deployment never owns market interpretation.**  
The Market Data Adapter translates structure only. It assigns no meaning to price, volume, or time.

**Deployment remains stateless.**  
All deployment components hold no mutable state between calls. State resides exclusively in the Execution Domain.

**Deployment never stores behavioural history.**  
No timeline, window, or historical accumulation exists in the Deployment Layer. Behavioural history remains exclusively in the Behavioural Reality domain.

**Deployment never modifies frozen objects.**  
All domain objects flowing through the pipeline are immutable. Deployment components treat them as read-only inputs.

**Deployment coordinates only.**  
Every Deployment component is a coordinator, translator, assembler, or factory. None is a decision-maker.

## 1. Executive Summary

QuantForge possesses a permanently frozen scientific engine (Phases 1–7) capable of observing market behaviour, interpreting it, and executing deterministic trades. However, the architecture currently assumes that research arrives already validated and pre-configured for deployment. 

Phase 8 exists to industrialize behavioural research. It establishes the permanent constitutional framework required to standardize the transition of a behavioural hypothesis from an idea, through rigorous scientific validation, into a deterministic deployment, and finally into empirical feedback that seeds future research. Phase 8 does not introduce new trading strategies, nor does it redesign the frozen Behavioral Observation Engine (BOE). It builds the factory that feeds the BOE and the loop that learns from it.

## 2. Constitutional Purpose

The role of Phase 8 inside QuantForge is to close the scientific lifecycle loop. It mandates that the platform operates as a continuous cycle rather than a linear pipeline.

Phase 8 connects the operational state of the system back to the research state, ensuring that:

```
Research
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Deployment
   ↓
Feedback
   ↓
Research
```

> Historical terminology note: "Feedback" here is the planning-era label for the
> closing lifecycle stage. The frozen stage name is **Deployment Evidence Capture**,
> terminating at the read-only `OutcomeReader` service with Human Research Review
> outside QuantForge software (see `PHASE8_PERMANENT_FREEZE.md`).

Without Phase 8, QuantForge is a deterministic execution engine. With Phase 8, QuantForge becomes a Scientific Operating System capable of repeatedly discovering, validating, and deploying behavioural edges while preserving the immutability of the core engine.

## 3. Scientific Philosophy

Phase 8 enforces strict separation across the scientific lifecycle:

**Research creates hypotheses.** It defines what market behaviour is believed to exist and how it should be measured. Research does not execute.
**Validation proves hypotheses.** It tests the research against deterministic replay and immutable evidence. Validation does not interpret; it evaluates.
**Deployment executes validated hypotheses.** It wires proven scientific components into a deterministic runtime. Deployment does not learn.
**Feedback creates new hypotheses.** It records the empirical outcome of deployment as fresh evidence. Feedback does not alter history; it informs the future.

Every stage is independent. Research cannot deploy. Deployment cannot validate. Validation cannot execute. Feedback cannot rewrite established evidence. This independence guarantees that the scientific integrity of the platform remains uncompromised by operational pressures.

Phase 8 is strictly bound by the frozen architecture of Phases 1–7. Phase 8 may NEVER:

- Modify the Behavioral Observation Engine (BOE).
- Modify any frozen public contract or domain object.
- Bypass the Scientific Validation domain.
- Deploy unvalidated research into the operational runtime.
- Perform automated parameter optimization.
- Perform AI-based market prediction.
- Automatically rewrite or alter frozen strategies based on live performance.
- Introduce hidden business logic into the deterministic pipeline.

## 6. Canonical Lifecycle

The canonical lifecycle of a behavioural edge in QuantForge is permanently frozen as:

```
Research Candidate
        ↓
Scientific Validation
        ↓
Validated Behaviour
        ↓
Strategy Manifest
        ↓
Deployment Runtime
        ↓
Execution
        ↓
Feedback
        ↓
Research Candidate
```

- **Research Candidate:** The formal, immutable declaration of a behavioural hypothesis and its required observational components.
- **Scientific Validation:** The deterministic replay of the Research Candidate against historical market data to produce immutable Evidence and Validation outcomes.
- **Validated Behaviour:** A validated scientific truth that has met the statistical and reproducibility criteria of the Research Protocol.
- **Strategy Manifest:** The deterministic wiring configuration that binds the Validated Behaviour to the frozen BOE registries.
- **Deployment Runtime:** The frozen Phase 7 operational shell that executes the Strategy Manifest.
- **Execution:** The physical or simulated market interaction resulting in an immutable outcome.
- **Feedback:** The capture of execution outcomes and market observations as new, immutable scientific evidence appended to the historical record.

## 7. Phase 8 Components

Phase 8 introduces the following constitutional concepts. Their implementations may vary, but their responsibilities are frozen:

**Research Candidate**
Responsible for structuring an idea into a testable scientific claim with defined market scope, expected behaviour, and invalidation criteria.

**Scientific Validation Framework**
Responsible for executing the Research Candidate against the frozen Replay Engine, collecting Evidence, and deterministically evaluating the outcome without interpreting it.

**Strategy Manifest**
Responsible for declaratively mapping a Validated Behaviour to the specific, frozen Observers, Policies, and Risk Models that comprise its operational composition.

**Deployment Assembly**
Responsible for consuming the Strategy Manifest and assembling the frozen Phase 7 Deployment Runtime deterministically. It performs no logic.

**Operational Feedback**
Responsible for extracting live execution outcomes and associated behavioural timelines, and appending them to the permanent Experiment Ledger as new scientific evidence.

## 8. Guiding Principles

The following principles are permanent rules for Phase 8:

- **Validation before Deployment:** No behavioural edge may enter the Deployment Runtime without surviving Scientific Validation.
- **Deployment generates Research:** Every deployment is a new scientific experiment. Its outcome is research data, not an optimization target.
- **Research must be reproducible:** Every hypothesis must be testable against historical replay with identical results.
- **Everything is deterministic:** No stage of the research lifecycle may rely on dynamic time, randomness, or hidden state.
- **Scientific evidence outranks opinion:** Empirical feedback may challenge a hypothesis, but historical evidence cannot be rewritten to match new outcomes.
- **Configuration outranks hardcoding:** Strategy components are wired via Manifests, not code modifications.

## 10. Relationship to Previous Phases

Phase 8 extends the frozen architecture without modifying it:

- It consumes the **Phase 2 Replay Engine** to power Scientific Validation.
- It utilizes the **Phase 3, 4, and 5 Registries** to resolve Strategy Manifests.
- It relies on the **Phase 6 Validation Contracts** to conclude experiments.
- It injects into the **Phase 7 Deployment Bootstrap** to assemble the runtime.
- It writes to the **Phase 1 Experiment Ledger** to record Operational Feedback.

Phase 8 is the connective tissue that binds the frozen components into a continuous loop.

## 11. Definition of Success

Phase 8 succeeds when a behavioural hypothesis can move from idea, to validation, to deployment, to feedback, and back to research, without ever requiring a modification to the frozen BOE architecture, the domain objects, or the public contracts. A behavioural edge reaches deployment because it has survived scientific validation—not because it appears profitable.

## 12. Constitutional Rules

1. Research cannot bypass Validation.
2. Deployment cannot modify Research.
3. Feedback cannot modify historical evidence.
4. Every deployment must possess scientific provenance.
5. Every validated behavioural edge must be reproducible via replay.
6. The pipeline remains downward-only; operational results never mutate scientific definitions.

Future phases may extend Phase 8 by introducing new research tools, broader validation scopes, or advanced deployment monitoring. Future phases may NOT weaken Phase 8 by removing the separation between research, validation, deployment, and feedback.
