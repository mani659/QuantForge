# QUANTFORGE — DUAL-PATH STRATEGY AND SYSTEM GOVERNANCE
# RESEARCH FACTORY V2 AMENDMENT
# DATE: 2026-08-27

## 1. Purpose
This governance artifact amends the QuantForge Research Factory doctrine so that the final trading architecture may legitimately emerge through two distinct paths: a Single Killer Strategy or a Modular Multi-Artifact System. This establishes the formal rules for component qualification and assembly, ensuring that system combination is never used to rescue failed research.

## 2. Single Strategy Path (Path A)
One research artifact independently demonstrates sufficient scientific support, economic expectancy, robustness, executable viability, and forward validation to become the primary strategy architecture.
**Flow:** Standalone Candidate → Qualification → Forward Validation → Bot

## 3. Modular System Path (Path B)
Multiple independently meaningful research artifacts are eventually assembled into a single system whose combined properties provide positive expectancy, diversified opportunity flow, improved drawdown behavior, acceptable transaction costs, and reduced dependence on any one event.
**Flow:** Component Candidate → Scientific Qualification → Economic Qualification → System Assembly → System Validation → Bot

## 4. Standalone vs Component Evaluation
Every sufficiently interesting artifact is evaluated on two separate axes:
- **Standalone Role:** Can this artifact operate as an independent strategy? (Standalone-qualified, marginal standalone, not standalone viable).
- **Component Role:** Does this artifact provide independently meaningful expected value or diversification that could be useful inside a future system? (Qualified component candidate, component candidate, not useful, disqualified).
STANDALONE INSUFFICIENCY DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY DISQUALIFY A VALID COMPONENT. When adjudicating, we must ask: "Did it fail because the artifact itself is economically/scientifically invalid, or because it is merely insufficient as a standalone strategy?" Only the latter may remain eligible for component consideration.

## 5. Component Eligibility
A candidate may be retained as a `COMPONENT-CANDIDATE` only when:
- The event is independently meaningful.
- The economics are positive or materially useful.
- The behavior is not a statistical accident.
- Its limitations are explicitly documented.
- It has not been scientifically contradicted.
- It does not depend on rescuing an already-failed hypothesis.
- Its intended system role is clear.

## 6. Closed/Failed Research Firewall
The following may NOT be resurrected through system assembly:
- Scientifically contradicted research.
- Negative-expectancy artifacts.
- Treatment conditions whose registered counterfactual is superior (e.g., CAND-055).
- Invalid implementations.
- Data-blocked objects (unless separately reintroduced through G0).
- Patterns rejected solely through an explicit and frozen falsification test.
**SYSTEM ASSEMBLY MAY DIVERSIFY QUALIFIED RESEARCH; IT MAY NOT RESUSCITATE FAILED RESEARCH.**

## 7. Qualification Levels
- **COMPONENT-CANDIDATE:** Interesting artifact retained for possible future system investigation.
- **SCIENTIFICALLY QUALIFIED COMPONENT:** Mechanism/effect has survived the required scientific validation.
- **ECONOMICALLY QUALIFIED COMPONENT:** Economic viability has survived the required economic validation.
- **SYSTEM-QUALIFIED COMPONENT:** The artifact has independently qualified AND survived the System Assembly selection gate.

## 8. System Assembly Requirements
System Assembly MUST NOT begin simply because several component candidates exist. Before S0, each candidate must have documented: Provenance, Research Status, Economic Status, Scientific Status, Limitations, and System Role.

## 9. System Assembly Metrics
When Assembly is authorized, it must be evaluated on:
- Opportunity Union
- Overlap
- Return Correlation
- Conditional Dependence
- Cost Scaling
- Expectancy Contribution
- Drawdown Contribution
- Concentration
- Regime Coverage
- Failure Cascades

## 10. Rescue Prohibition
Combination cannot turn FAILED into COMPONENT. If an artifact has negative net expectancy or its condition degrades the counterfactual opportunity, combining it with three other losers will not create a winner.

## 11. Frequency Doctrine
LOW FREQUENCY IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY A KILL CONDITION. We ask: "Is the frequency appropriate for the artifact's intended role?" A core strategy requires recurring opportunity, while an event opportunist (e.g., CAND-035) or diversifier may legitimately be very rare.
