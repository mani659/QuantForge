# QuantForge — RESEARCH FACTORY V2
# CONDITIONAL BEHAVIOR PRINCIPLE & OPPORTUNITY INTEGRITY V1

## 1. Objective

This document refines the core objective of the QuantForge Research Factory V2 based on the methodological failure observed during the CAND-010 event-state duplication anomaly. The goal is to explicitly define the project's primary scientific aim: discovering positive mathematical expectancy derived from conditional post-event market behavior, and enforcing strict structural rules ("Opportunity Integrity") to prevent event duplication in state-based candidates.

## 2. Pattern vs Event vs Opportunity vs Behavior vs Edge

To avoid conflating "chart patterns" with economic realities, QuantForge adopts strict terminology:

- **Pattern:** An observable, recurring geometric or structural configuration/state.
- **Event:** A specific, historical occurrence of that pattern/state.
- **Opportunity:** A new, non-duplicated event that triggers an executable decision.
- **Behavior:** The conditional market response that unfolds in the forward time window immediately following the event.
- **Edge:** A persistent directional or statistical asymmetry within that conditional response.
- **Economic Edge:** An edge whose executable mathematical expectancy remains positive and meaningful after accounting for realistic trading friction.

The fundamental research pipeline is:
> **Pattern/Event → Conditional Behavior → Executable Expectancy → Economic Edge**

The goal is NOT to find a pattern that yields a 100% win rate. It is to discover conditional behaviors that skew a return distribution into net positive expectancy.

## 3. Mathematical Expectancy

The research factory evaluates mathematical expectancy, not accuracy alone. For directional candidates, future validation MUST consider:

> **E[R] = P(win) × average win − P(loss) × average loss − friction**

An opportunity with a 45% win rate can be economically exceptional if its payoff structure is highly asymmetric. A 70% win rate can be economically disastrous if friction devours the small wins. G1 and G2 screens exist conceptually to approximate whether E[R] survives transaction costs.

## 4. Opportunity Integrity

A mandatory, lightweight structural check applied to all event-based candidates before execution. It prevents single market episodes from creating dozens of artificial trades. Every candidate definition MUST explicitly address:

1. **Event onset:** The exact bar/tick when the event becomes logically true.
2. **Event completion:** When the event ends or expires.
3. **Re-arm condition:** What structural reset must occur before a subsequent opportunity can be triggered.
4. **Duplicate suppression:** How the logic ignores repeated/persistent bars representing the exact same ongoing event.
5. **Overlap rule:** How simultaneous or overlapping opportunities are managed.
6. **Executable entry:** When the actual trade decision becomes available.

## 5. Conditional KPI Discovery

QuantForge does not just blindly test static patterns. The highest value research investigates which observable market conditions (KPIs) influence the probability, direction, magnitude, or persistence of the post-event response. 

Future candidates should explicitly seek to answer:
> **What event repeatedly occurs, what does price tend to do after it, and what observable state/KPI changes that conditional response?**

Examples of conditional KPIs include:
- Volatility state / Relative volatility
- Cross-market state
- Session timing
- Momentum / Trend state
- Liquidity state
- Distance from major structural levels

*Note: These are hypotheses, not facts. Arbitrary feature-mining is strictly prohibited. KPIs must possess a plausible mechanism and be frozen before execution.*

## 6. SMC Integration

Owner-supplied Smart Money Concepts (SMC) are recognized as domain hypotheses about repeatable market behavior and possible mechanisms. They are legitimate inputs for candidate generation because they claim to identify repeating structures that create conditional directional distributions.

SMC claims are NOT accepted as proven facts. They will neither be dismissed due to their terminology nor accepted merely because of their popularity. They will be mechanically frozen and empirically measured.

## 7. CAND-010 Lesson

- **Status:** INVALID / NON-ADJUDICABLE
- **Lesson:** The G1 script for CAND-010 successfully identified an HTF Sweep followed by a CHOCH. However, the state-machine generated overlapping duplicate opportunities for every subsequent M5 bar that remained inside the sweep zone while breaching a minor swing low. This methodological failure perfectly illustrates the necessity of the **Opportunity Integrity** requirement: *Event persistence must not be counted as repeated independent opportunities.*

## 8. CAND-011 Status

- **Status:** ECONOMICALLY INSUFFICIENT
- **Result:** The candidate correctly isolated valid double-top structures with RSI divergence. However, the gross return over the 4-hour forward window was negligible (+0.16 bps median), completely failing to exceed realistic friction (3.0 bps). The hypothesis was formally falsified as a standalone directional edge.

## 9. Research Factory Implications

No new heavy infrastructure or governance stages will be created to enforce these rules. The Factory remains lightweight and cheap. Opportunity Integrity and KPI conditioning must be simple, deterministic components of the frozen G0 candidate definition itself.
