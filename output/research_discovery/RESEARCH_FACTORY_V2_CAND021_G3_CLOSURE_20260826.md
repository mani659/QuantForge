## 1. Candidate
**CAND-G0-021** — Pre-Auction Liquidity Vacuum Reversal Conditioned by Volatility Regime.
This candidate advanced through G0, G1, and G2 based on a hypothesis that the pre-entry D1 volatility state could distinguish favorable liquidity-vacuum reversals from adverse macro repricing events.

## 2. G3 Result
**CLOSED — SCIENTIFICALLY CONTRADICTED**
The formal G3 scientific validation demonstrated that the pre-entry D1 volatility state did NOT meaningfully alter the post-entry response relative to the comparison population.

## 3. Statistical Evidence
- **Primary Effect (FAVORABLE − ADVERSE):** -2.09 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [-38.90, +35.17]
- **Two-sided Permutation p-value:** 0.8991 (10,000 permutations, seed 42)
The hypothesis that the FAVORABLE state yields a superior mean return was completely rejected by the data. The observed difference was statistically indistinguishable from a random assignment of state labels.

## 4. Temporal Evidence
- **Development Difference:** -4.15 points
- **Holdout Difference:** +3.61 points
The conditional effect lacked sign stability across the chronological split, further confirming that the observed variance was driven by unhedged macro shocks randomly clustering, rather than a genuine conditional state phenomenon.

## 5. Scientific Classification
**CONTRADICTED.** The apparent economic separation observed in G1/G2 was not supported as a genuine conditional scientific effect.

## 6. Why G4 Was Not Performed
G4 (Economic Validation) requires a scientifically validated object. Because the G3 permutation test failed to reject the null hypothesis, advancing the contradicted object to G4 would constitute economic data mining rather than scientific validation. 

## 7. Methodological Lesson
**Descriptive subgroup profitability does not establish a genuine conditional effect.**
G1 and G2 may legitimately identify an economically interesting subgroup with a positive mean/median. However, a subgroup cannot be promoted to scientific support solely because its absolute return is positive. Scientific validation must rigorously test: *Does the conditioning variable itself separate outcomes relative to an appropriate comparator or null?*
This distinction between *descriptive subgroup profitability* and a *genuine conditional scientific effect* is now a permanent Research Factory governance rule.
