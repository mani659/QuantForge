## 5. Conditional-Value Lesson
A structurally observable confirmation condition can reduce predictive value even when the underlying event appears profitable. (e.g., Demanding a Market Structure Shift degraded the pure sweep).

## 6. Rescue-as-New-Hypothesis Rule
When a treatment fails because its condition is inferior to its counterfactual, removing that condition is a new hypothesis, not a rescue.

## 7. Sample-Size Lesson
Very small samples cannot establish component value even when per-event economics are attractive. (e.g., CAND-058 had massive per-event economics but N=2 renders it entirely non-adjudicable).

## 8. SMC Structural-State Lesson
SMC structural terminology should be translated into independently testable state transitions; one failed transition (e.g., Sweep + MSS) does not invalidate the entire structural framework.
