## 2. V3 Framework Applied as Audit Lens

The audit applies the ratified G1 V3 framework as a retrospective lens:

**Hard Validity Gates (9):** Deterministic definition, executable entry, no hindsight, correct cost normalization, data integrity, legitimate counterfactual, causal claims limited to observables, no future-bar dependency, reproducibility.

**Economic Evidence:** Mean, median, dispersion, tail behavior, counterfactual delta, N, frequency, per-event economics — all treated as evidence, not thresholds.

**Four Artifact Classes:** Standalone Alpha, Rare-Event Alpha, State/Condition, Regime/Specialist.

**Evidence Profiles:** Economically Negative, Informationally Interesting, Economically Promising, Qualification-Worthy, Rare-Event Qualification-Worthy.
