## A. RATIFICATION DECLARATION

**V38A BASE VALIDATION PATHWAY RATIFIED.** QuantForge adopts the Base Validation Pathway and its governing clauses BV1–BV13 (Section D) as the authoritative procedure by which a future decision process may earn BASE status under V38. The ratification preserves the governing balance: a Base does NOT need to be Qualified Alpha; a Base cannot be an arbitrary or pathological losing process retained because Conditional research might rescue it; and economic metrics are mandatory evidence but are NOT universal Base-eligibility thresholds. **No Base is selected, created, or hypothesized. No Base validation occurs. The Base Registry remains EMPTY. No experiment is executed.**

## 4. MANDATORY SEMANTICS — BASE DEFINITION

**Base** = a registered, deterministic decision process determining actual trade participation/execution under a declared scope, data source, outcome definition, execution model, and cost model. Explicit non-identities: observation ≠ Base; state/event ≠ Base; hypothesis ≠ Base; Conditional ≠ Base; Qualified Alpha ≠ Base. CAND-077, CAND-081, CAND-083, and CAND-099 remain observations and do not become Base candidates by reinterpretation.

## 5. BASE VIABILITY

Ratified: **No sign, magnitude, or threshold of net expectancy independently determines Base eligibility.** Economic metrics are mandatory evidence. No universal Base gate may be created from net/gross expectancy, cost floor, win rate, drawdown, Sharpe, volatility, trade count, sample count, p-values, confidence intervals, frequency, minimum edge, minimum return, or maximum loss. These may be recorded and interpreted as evidence; they may not become automatic Base pass/fail criteria.

## 6. VIABILITY CATEGORIES

**BASE-ELIGIBLE** — the complete evidence establishes the process is: structurally valid; deterministic and reproducible; prospectively defined; economically coherent; non-pathological; executable in its declared context; supported by a credible frozen opportunity population; interpretable as an independent reference decision context; independent of Conditional-component results; and not constructed or retained as a rescue vehicle.

**INCONCLUSIVE** — evidence is insufficient to establish legitimate Base-reference status, including unresolved reproducibility, execution realism, data sufficiency, opportunity-population adequacy, scope integrity, interpretability, or evidence independence. Borderline cases are adjudicated holistically without numerical boundaries.

**NOT BASE-ELIGIBLE** — affirmative evidence of structural or conceptual invalidity, including leakage; irreproducibility; pathological behavior; incoherent decision economics; post-hoc population construction; post-hoc selection; rescue construction; implausible execution; Conditional dependence. No "positive = eligible" or "negative = ineligible" rule is reintroduced.

## 7. ANTI-ALPHA-LITE

Ratified: Base status is **not** a weaker form of Qualified Alpha. A Base receives no standalone profitability claim. A Base may have positive, approximately neutral, or negative measured economics; the sign itself does not determine status. BASE = legitimate reference decision context; QUALIFIED ALPHA = standalone economic qualification under G1 V3 (unchanged).

## 8. MOTIVATION ≠ VALIDATION

Ratified: motivating evidence cannot simultaneously serve as validating evidence where doing so contaminates prospective integrity. A motivated Base hypothesis requires a genuinely independent confirmation segment and/or genuinely new observations not part of the motivating evidence. No universal duration or sample-size rule is created. If sufficient independent validation evidence does not exist: **DATA-INSUFFICIENT** — no Registry entry occurs.

## 9. SIX-STAGE BASE PATHWAY

Ratified, exactly six stages:
```text
Stage 0 — Hypothesis formulation
Stage 1 — Prospective registration
Stage 2 — Structural validation
Stage 3 — Economic validation
Stage 4 — Viability adjudication
Stage 5 — Registry entry and lifecycle initiation
```
Stage 2 precedes exposure to economic outcomes. Stage 3 measures the registered decision economics. Stage 4 performs holistic viability adjudication. Stage 5 is the actual Registry-entry boundary.

## 10. ECONOMIC VALIDATION

Ratified: Stage 3 measures gross economics; net economics; the frozen cost model; distributional diagnostics; trade frequency; and any other predeclared diagnostics — over the complete registered scope. Post-hoc favorable subsets cannot replace the primary registered population.

## 11. OPPORTUNITY POPULATION

Ratified distinction: **Base opportunity population** (complete population generated by the frozen Base decision rule) vs **assembled decision eligibility** (the later question of which Base opportunities a Conditional allows, modifies, or rejects under a predeclared assembly design). Conditional components may modify action and may modify confirmation/execution where predeclared; they may NOT redefine the Base universe and may NOT retroactively remove unfavorable Base opportunities. The Base population is frozen and is the reference substrate for assembled attribution.

## 12. COST / EXECUTION GOVERNANCE

Ratified: one coherent cost model per Base; cost model frozen at registration; realistic for the declared execution context; no opportunistic cost adjustment; assembled testing inherits the Base execution/cost context unless a separately ratified governance change permits otherwise. **The cost model measures execution realism; it is not a universal profitability gate.**

## 13. NO-RESCUE

Ratified (structural anti-rescue doctrine): a Base may not be admitted because "Conditional research might fix it." A Conditional experiment may only ask whether a Conditional improves a legitimate reference decision — never whether it rescues a failed process. Rescue-framed registrations or tests are invalid. SEED-002 remains the historical governance lesson supporting this boundary.

## 14. BASE REGISTRY

Ratified: Registry entry requires the complete pathway — prospective registration → structural validity → economic evidence → reproducibility/interpretability evidence → holistic viability adjudication → owner decision → registry entry. Entry confers authorization for assembled research referencing the Base. It does NOT confer standalone profitability, Qualified Alpha status, or production tradeability. **The Base Registry remains EMPTY immediately after this ratification.**

## 15. VERSIONING / CLOSURE

Ratified: material decision-rule change, scope change, population-definition change, cost-model change, or outcome-definition change → new Base hypothesis and validation. Demonstrable outcome-independent implementation bug → governed patch with provenance. Closure → owner decision with recorded reason. Closed Base return → only through fresh prospective governance. Historical Base status may not be edited to absorb later evidence.

## 16. EXISTING RESEARCH DISPOSITION

Ratified preservation: CAND-077/081/083/099 are not Bases; H01 remains CLOSED; ORD remains CLOSED; TRADEABLE_EDGE candidates remain governed by existing closure/family rules; no closed research is reopened; prior results may motivate future hypotheses but do not confer Base status.

## 17. HTF → LTF

Ratified compatibility with an internally hierarchical Base (HTF context → structural condition → LTF trigger → LTF confirmation → execution), provided the entire sequence is deterministic, prospectively frozen, and validated as one decision process. Internal frozen layers are part of the Base; anything added after registration to condition the Base is a Conditional component.

## 18. LIQUIDITY RESEARCH

Ratified only architectural compatibility. No liquidity definitions, zones, thresholds, sweep rules, execution rules, or experiments are created by this milestone. A future liquidity-based Base would require a fresh hypothesis and full governed validation.

## 19. BASE REGISTRY IMPLEMENTATION

Not implemented. Registry implementation remains a later milestone. This ratification freezes the governance pathway only.

## D. ADOPTED BV1–BV13 — FINAL GOVERNING CLAUSES

The following clauses are adopted verbatim from the ratified design (V1-r2, Section 24) as the governing Base-validation clauses. (R = requirement, P = prohibition, W = rationale, G = governance consequence.)

**BV1 — Base definition.** R: A Base is a registered, deterministic decision process determining actual trade participation/execution under a declared scope, data source, outcome definition, execution model, and cost model. P: No observation, state, event, indicator, historical subset, hypothetical P&L, or post-hoc combination may be a Base. W: Only real decision processes can be increment references. G: Object-type check at hypothesis formulation; non-decision-process objects are ineligible by construction.

**BV2 — Base legitimacy.** R: A Base hypothesis records its decision logic, motivation (including any prior-result citation), and proposed scope before any measurement. P: Retrospective subset creation; favorable-period scoping; motivation ≠ validation violations. W: Legitimacy is established by prospective record, not outcome. G: Unregistered measurement is void.

**BV3 — Prospective registration.** R: Registration freezes all REQUIRED registration fields (instrument, scope, decision rule, population, cost model, outcome, execution assumptions, validation procedure, motivation) pre-measurement; any post-measurement field change invalidates the study. P: Scope, parameter, or population changes after outcome exposure. W: The freeze is the anti-retrospection mechanism. G: Deviations close the study as NOT BASE-ELIGIBLE.

**BV4 — Structural validation.** R: Stage 2 establishes determinism, reproducibility, leakage-freedom, and valid population construction; no economic outcomes are exposed. P: Economic exposure before structural sign-off. W: Structure must precede economics. G: Structural failure closes the study as NOT BASE-ELIGIBLE.

**BV5 — Economic validation (economics as evidence, not gates).** R: Stage 3 measures the registered decision economics — gross and net under the frozen cost model, distributional diagnostics, trade frequency — over the full predeclared scope. Economic metrics are required evidence for holistic viability adjudication; they are not universal qualification thresholds. No sign, magnitude, or threshold of net or gross expectancy, win rate, drawdown, volatility, Sharpe, sample count, or p-value is a Base-eligibility rule. P: Subset reporting as primary evidence; post-hoc metric selection; treating any single economic statistic as an automatic pass/fail. W: Economics are evidence; interpretability and structure adjudicate. G: Metrics are predeclared in the registration and interpreted holistically.

**BV6 — Viability adjudication (holistic).** R: Stage 4 classifies BASE-ELIGIBLE, INCONCLUSIVE, or NOT BASE-ELIGIBLE by total evidence against the viability dimensions (structural validity; determinism and reproducibility; prospective definition; economic coherence; non-pathology; execution realism; credible frozen opportunity population; interpretability as a reference decision context; independence from Conditional-component results; not a rescue vehicle). No sign, magnitude, or threshold of net expectancy independently determines the classification. Borderline cases are adjudicated on the complete dimensions, with the adjudicator documenting why the process is sufficiently interpretable as a Base, insufficiently established, or affirmatively invalid. P: Automatic status rules keyed to positive/zero/negative economics; entry on weak evidence; deliberate selection of an arbitrary or deeply pathological losing process as a research anchor. W: The viability tension is resolved by dimensional coherence and interpretability, not by a profit or loss bar. G: Classification is an independent adjudication-review recommendation; the owner decides entry.

**BV7 — Motivation–validation separation and data freshness.** R: Motivating observations may not serve as validating observations; motivated hypotheses require a genuinely independent confirmation segment and/or genuinely new observations not part of the motivating evidence; if neither exists, the study is DATA-INSUFFICIENT. Sufficiency is adjudicated per declared scope, data availability, and evidence independence — no universal duration or sample threshold. P: Reusing motivating results as validation. W: Prospective integrity requires validation independent of discovery. G: DATA-INSUFFICIENT studies are recorded, not entered.

**BV8 — Base registry entry (no implicit profitability gate).** R: Entry requires structural validity + reproducibility-and-interpretability evidence + economically coherent and non-pathological decision economics (as evidence) + execution realism + prospective integrity, adjudicated holistically and approved by the owner; the entry record fixes the decision rule, scope, cost model, population, and validation reference. The entry chain encodes no implicit profitability gate. P: Entry on a single economic statistic; entry by comparative post-hoc selection among parallel candidates; entry of rescue vehicles or arbitrary/pathological processes. W: Entry is a governed mutation establishing a reference context, not a profitability certification. G: Registry entry authorizes assembled research referencing the Base; it confers no standalone tradeability claim.

**BV9 — Registry protections.** R: The entry process prevents retroactive insertion, favorable-period insertion, post-hoc scope/parameter change, subset carving, conditional-component contamination of Base definition, and parallel-winner selection. P: Any of the foregoing. W: Protections keep the reference decision clean. G: Violations void the affected Base record.

**BV10 — Versioning, modification, closure.** R: Implementation bug fixes are patched against the registration with outcome-independent provenance; methodological modifications open a new Base version/study; redesign opens a new hypothesis; closure is an owner decision with recorded reason; a closed Base returns only via a new prospective hypothesis. P: Editing historical Base status to reflect new results. W: Material change = new evidence, not new history. G: Records are append-only.

**BV11 — Opportunity-population freeze.** R: The Base's opportunity population is constructed at Stage 2 under the frozen rule and fixed at registry entry as the exclusive substrate for assembled comparisons; the Base population definition and assembled-decision eligibility are distinct concepts; components may condition decisions but never redefine the universe or retroactively remove Base opportunities. P: Component-driven population redefinition or retroactive removal. W: Attribution requires a stable reference universe. G: Population definition is part of the entry record.

**BV12 — Conditional eligibility dependency.** R: No assembled economic test and no conditional qualification occurs without a Base registry entry; conditional observations may be characterized and preserved regardless. P: Assembled testing against unvalidated references or processes disqualified at adjudication. W: The Base requirement is the anti-phantom and anti-rescue structure (V38 D8). G: Experiments without a registry entry are void.

**BV13 — Anti-rescue protection (structural, not threshold-based).** R: Base status may never be used as a rescue mechanism: a process proposed or retained for the purpose of being "fixed" by Conditional research is NOT BASE-ELIGIBLE, and arbitrary or deeply pathological losing processes cannot qualify simply because a Conditional might later improve them. Disqualification operates through the structural dimensions — rescue construction, pathology, incoherent decision economics, irreproducibility, implausible execution — not through a sign or magnitude rule. The primary question of any assembled test remains whether a predeclared Conditional improves a legitimate reference decision; SEED-002 is the recorded lesson. P: Rescue framing in any registration or test; proposing a Base because it is deeply negative. W: Anti-rescue must be structural so that no gate can be gamed and no sign rule can exclude a legitimate reference. G: Rescue-framed registrations are rejected at admission; rescue-framed tests are void.

## F. RATIFICATION BOUNDARY

**This ratification DOES:** adopt the Base Validation Pathway and BV1–BV13 as governing clauses; fix the canonical Base definition, viability framework, six-stage lifecycle, economic-adjudication rule, opportunity-population rule, cost/execution rule, and versioning/closure rule; establish that the Base Registry remains EMPTY; set the next milestone to Base Hypothesis Selection / Validation Design.

**This ratification DOES NOT:** select, create, or hypothesize a Base; implement the Base Registry; execute Base validation; admit any Conditional component; execute assembled or conditional testing; reopen any closed research; modify G1 V3, V38, or the System Assembly Candidate Register; authorize validation of H01, ORD, CAND-077/081/083/099, or any TRADEABLE_EDGE candidate.
