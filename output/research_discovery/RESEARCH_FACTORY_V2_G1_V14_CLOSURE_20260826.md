## 5. Component-Candidate vs Qualified-Component Doctrine
To protect the integrity of System Assembly, QuantForge enforces the following distinction:
- **COMPONENT-CANDIDATE:** A historical artifact with sufficiently interesting per-event economic evidence to justify retention for potential future investigation. (e.g., CAND-042). This does NOT mean scientifically validated.
- **QUALIFIED SYSTEM COMPONENT:** A component that has independently satisfied the rigorous chronological and scientific evidence required by the System Assembly gate (G2/G3).
- **Rule:** A Component-Candidate cannot be assembled into a live system until it satisfies the independent scientific qualification process.

## 6. Counterfactual Lesson
CAND-042 provided a critical lesson:
> A highly profitable event is not sufficient evidence for the proposed causal mechanism when the registered counterfactual is also profitable.
Because the divergent shock (counterfactual) also produced massive mean-reverting profits, the profitability of the correlated shock does not prove that forced liquidation caused it. Component retention may occur before mechanism validation, but System Assembly qualification requires stronger independent evidence.

## 7. Mechanism/Data Lesson
> A price artifact may be retained as a component candidate even when its proposed causal mechanism cannot currently be observed (e.g., due to missing positioning data).
But:
> The inability to observe the causal mechanism prevents scientific qualification and System Assembly qualification.
This preserves potentially valuable price behavior without overstating what the evidence proves.
