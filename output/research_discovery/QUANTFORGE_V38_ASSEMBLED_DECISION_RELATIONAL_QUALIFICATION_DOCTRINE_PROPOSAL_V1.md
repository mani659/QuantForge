# PART I — STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

## 1. EXECUTIVE DOCTRINE PROPOSAL

QuantForge shall adopt, subject to owner ratification of the clauses in Section 21, the **HYBRID BASE + CONDITIONAL** architecture for a new, explicitly governed class of research: the evaluation of conditional/event/state information as an *increment over a legitimate, independently validated Base decision*, under a single coherent execution and cost model. The Base Registry begins **EMPTY**. No historical artifact — CAND-077/081/083, CAND-099, H01 (including its contemporary-era cell), ORD, or any closed line — is a Base, and none may become one by governance declaration or retrospective subset selection. The doctrine's purpose is to make lawful one research question — *"does adding this predeclared conditional component improve this legitimate Base decision?"* — while rendering unlawful, by explicit rule, the three failure modes the record documents: retrospective rescue, permutation mining, and hypothetical P&L construction. **No relational experiment, no Base, no component, and no hypothesis is authorized by this document.**

## 2. STRATEGIC RATIONALE

1. (FACT) Standalone-first qualification has been measured to failure across three independent lines: ORD V1.1.0 (ECONOMICALLY NON-VIABLE, gross 1–2 bps vs spread), TRADEABLE_EDGE V27–V36 (0/20 valid standalone survivors, ≈0.5 bps gross wall at 2.0 bps friction), and H01 economic translation (ECONOMIC FAILURE overall).
2. (FACT) The conditional/state observations that survive (CAND-077 +1.67 bps; CAND-081 +1.23 bps; CAND-083 +4.60 bps mean deltas, all single-market) have never been given an economic gate, because the doctrine required a downstream Alpha that does not exist.
3. (FACT) The one assembled-adjacent test ever run — SEED-002 (CAND-083 × CAND-081) — was framed as a *rescue* of an economically negative event class and returned CONTROL SUPERIOR (delta mean −1.78 bps). It is the repository's proof that rescue framing fails and that State × State without a decision target is poorly posed.
4. (FACT) The strongest structural evidence that decision context carries economic information is H01's era-dependence: an identical pre-registered signal was strongly negative historically (−0.14%/day) and positive contemporarily (+0.078%/day, base and stress PASS) — the signal did not change, the context did.
5. (INFERENCE) Real trading systems are assembled from base decisions and conditional layers whose standalone expectancy may be ≈0. The question the program has never asked with a real base attached — *does this observable improve a decision?* — is distinct from *is this observable profitable?* and is the only research form never yet given an economic gate.
6. (INFERENCE) A Base × Conditional architecture, gated by an empty registry, prospective validation, increment-over-Base qualification, depth limits, predeclared pairings, and inherited firewalls, can investigate relationships without degenerating into combination mining.

# PART II — PROPOSED OBJECT MODEL AND ARCHITECTURE

## 4. PROPOSED OBJECT MODEL

The object vocabulary of the Relational Framework V1 (State, Event, Condition, Relational Hypothesis, Incremental Information, Interaction) is retained. Two new object types are proposed:

| Object | Definition | Standalone expectancy required? | Own validation required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BASE** | A registered, deterministic decision process that determines trade participation/execution under a declared scope, opportunity population, execution model, and cost model | No (need not be qualified Alpha); must satisfy qualitative **Base viability** (Clause D2) | YES — independent prospective Base validation |
| **ASSEMBLED DECISION** | Base + predeclared conditional component(s) + one execution model + one cost model; the unit of economic qualification | n/a | YES — increment test |
| CONDITIONAL COMPONENT | Observable/event/state that may modify a Base decision | NO — may be zero or negative | Characterization + increment evidence |
| OUTCOME | The measured economic result of a decision | n/a | n/a |

A full role taxonomy (REGIME SPECIALIST, EVENT OPPORTUNIST, etc.) already exists in the Assembly Register for production components and is not duplicated here. (INFERENCE) The minimal vocabulary addition is BASE and ASSEMBLED DECISION.

## 5. BASE DEFINITION

(Clause D1.) A BASE is a registered decision process that actually determines trade participation/execution. It must be deterministic, explicitly registered, independently reproducible, forward-scoped, outcome-blind at registration, associated with a defined opportunity population, an execution model, a coherent cost model, and evaluated under a predeclared protocol. It must NOT be an indicator alone, a state observation, a historical subset, a post-hoc favorable period, a hypothetical P&L, a combination assembled after seeing results, or a closed experiment carved into a favorable component.

## 6. BASE VALIDATION PATHWAY

(Clause D2.) A Base earns registry entry only through a **new prospective Base-validation study**: independent registration; pre-declared scope justified on forward-looking execution grounds (recorded before measurement and ratifiable without reference to any historical result's sign); outcome-blind design; deterministic decision rule; reproducibility; realistic costs; measured decision economics; temporal/confirmation separation where applicable. **BASE VALIDATED** is distinct from **QUALIFIED ALPHA**: a validated Base is a legitimate decision context, not an independently "killer" Alpha. A validated Base must nevertheless satisfy qualitative **Base viability** — decision economics that are not deeply negative at the decision level under its registered cost model — so that the increment question tests improvement, not rescue. Base viability is adjudicated holistically (no universal numeric cut-off is created) by the owner on the recommendation of a governance/adjudication review following G1 V3 holistic-adjudication practice.

## 7. CONDITIONAL COMPONENT DOCTRINE

(Clause D3.) A CONDITIONAL COMPONENT is an observable/event/state admitted to modify a specific Base decision. It does not require positive standalone expectancy; it may have zero or negative standalone expectancy; it must have a deterministic definition, provenance, and survive the existing G0 validity discipline and Mechanism–Observable Challenge; it is admitted for a specific relational purpose; and it earns qualification only through demonstrated incremental value over its ratified Base. Explicitly prohibited is the inference "it failed standalone, therefore we will use it conditionally": standalone failure is neither disqualifying nor qualifying — a component's relational role must be justified by a mechanism rationale for the *interaction*, independent of the component's standalone record.

## 8. ASSEMBLED DECISION DOCTRINE

(Clause D5.) The ASSEMBLED DECISION — Base + predeclared conditional component(s) + one coherent execution model + one coherent cost model — is the unit of economic qualification. The comparison preserves the Base opportunity set as far as reasonably possible. The test answers: *does conditional information improve the actual Base decision?* It never answers: *does the conditional component make a hypothetical trade profitable?*

## 9. INCREMENTAL ECONOMIC QUALIFICATION

(Clause D6.) A conditional component qualifies by demonstrating incremental value in the comparison BASE vs BASE + COMPONENT on the Base's own decision population, outcome definition, and cost schedule. Value forms (improved mean outcome, improved risk profile, reduced adverse outcomes, improved expectancy, reduced drawdown, improved execution quality) are admissible **only as predeclared metrics**; no metric may be selected after outcome inspection. Default primary measure: the Base's own pre-declared economic outcome. No universal numeric threshold is created; qualification is adjudicated holistically under G1 V3 practice.

## 10. NO-RESCUE RULE

(Clause D8.) SEED-002 is the architectural lesson. Its registered question — *does CAND-083 rescue CAND-081 from negative economics?* — is declared an invalid primary question class. A conditional experiment must never be framed as a mechanism for rescuing an economically invalid Base. Base viability (Clause D2) is the structural guard: because an invalid Base cannot be registered, rescue engineering has no legal target.

# PART III — FORMAL PROPOSED DOCTRINE (Clauses D1–D25)

## 11. RELATIONAL HYPOTHESIS ADMISSION

(Clause D4.) A relational hypothesis declares: Component A; Component B (or Base + component); the declared temporal/structural relationship; mechanistic rationale; expected dependency; expected economic consequence; counterfactual; execution context. It must be predeclared, mechanism-motivated, temporally explicit where appropriate, falsifiable, economically testable, and bounded in complexity. No relationship is admitted solely because both components "looked interesting historically."

## 12. COMPLEXITY / PERMUTATION CONTROLS

(Clause D11 — depth; Clause D12 — permutation control; Clause D13 — closed-line; Clause D14 — protected-forward.)

- **Depth:** Level 1 (Base + one conditional component) is the default qualification unit. Level 2 (Base + two explicitly related components) is permitted only when each component's Level-1 increment is established and the two components' interaction carries its own mechanism rationale, under separate registration. Level 3+ is prohibited absent separate governance. Rationale: interpretability declines and the multiple-testing burden rises with depth; the HTF→LTF architecture is naturally depth-2 and must be *earned* through Level-1 increments first.
- **Permutation control:** exhaustive combination search is prohibited. Pairings must be mechanism-driven and predeclared. No post-hoc component selection, no permutation ranking after outcomes, no "best combination" search, no threshold search within combinations, no repeated retries. Relational hypotheses are admitted in **controlled predeclared batches** — a batch is the set of hypotheses registered together under one milestone governance review; the size of a batch is bounded qualitatively by mechanism diversity and family constraint, not by an arbitrary number, and no new batch of the same family opens without a distinct mechanism rationale or new evidence.
- **Closed-line firewall:** existing rules retained verbatim — closed candidates are not admissible as positive relational inputs and contribute negative knowledge only. A closed artifact may be referenced only through a newly governed hypothesis independently justified; no silent recycling.
- **Protected-forward firewall:** CAND-015, CAND-024, CAND-035 remain completely excluded from relational discovery and qualification. No performance inspection, no relational use, no indirect leakage.

## 13. TEMPORAL / HTF→LTF DOCTRINE

(Clause D15.) The HTF-Context → Structural/Event Condition → LTF Confirmation → Execution Decision pattern is admitted as a research architecture subject to this doctrine, not as a claim. Each layer requires: deterministic definition; timestamp ordering with no look-ahead (HTF context must be closed at the LTF decision timestamp); synchronization rules; explicit maximum temporal distance; event dependency; counterfactual; one final execution decision; one economic outcome. Practitioner terminology — POI, sweep, displacement, reclaim, FVG, liquidity — is not admissible by name; each must be operationalized into a deterministic observable before any future hypothesis may enter research.

## 14. LIQUIDITY-HYPOTHESIS COMPATIBILITY

(Clause D16.) A future hypothesis of price movement toward identifiable liquidity locations, with sequential interaction at those locations, is classified **FUTURE UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS**. This doctrine is capable of hosting it as a Level-1 or Level-2 relational hypothesis over a ratified Base, subject to full relational admission and the operationalization requirement of Clause D15. No liquidity thresholds, stop-cluster definitions, pending-order-zone rules, or sweep rules are created by this document.

## 15. CLOSED / PROTECTED FIREWALLS

See Clause D13 and Clause D14. The firewalls of the existing Relational Framework V1 (permanently excluded objects; closed candidates NOT ADMISSIBLE as positive inputs; protected-forward objects permanently excluded) are inherited verbatim and are not weakened by any clause herein.

## 16. H01 AND EXISTING SRE TREATMENT

(Clause D18 — H01; Clause D19 — SRE objects.)

- **H01:** remains closed under its original economic adjudication. The contemporary-era positive cell is preserved as evidence and may motivate a future hypothesis. It does NOT become a Base automatically. Any future modern-era Base-validation study must be newly registered, independently justified on forward-looking execution grounds, scoped before examining the new study's outcomes, outcome-blind, and must not be presented as reopening H01.
- **CAND-077 / CAND-081 / CAND-083:** historical evidence remains intact; existing lifecycle governance (STATE REVIEW ELIGIBLE — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED) is unchanged; they do not become Bases; they do not become assembled components automatically; future reuse requires the new relational admission path; SEED-002 remains negative knowledge and is not retested automatically. CAND-099 likewise is not reopened and formalization of its V37A distributional divergence (OBS-006) remains subject to a new governed G0.

## 17. G1 V3 / MOC COMPATIBILITY

(Clause D20 — G1 V3; Clause D21 — Mechanism–Observable Challenge.)

The doctrine preserves the G1 V3 pillars — VALIDITY = HARD GATES; ECONOMIC METRICS = EVIDENCE; QUALIFICATION = HOLISTIC ADJUDICATION — and creates no universal economic hard threshold. Relational qualification maps onto the existing pipeline with the smallest change:

| Stage | Existing content | Relational addition |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Hypothesis and relationship integrity | Relational admission review (mechanism for the interaction; predeclared pairing) |
| G1 | Assembled-decision validity (9 hard gates apply to the assembled test) + economic evidence | Increment-over-Base evidence as the economic measurement |
| Further | Holistic system-value adjudication | Assembled-decision system qualification |

The Mechanism–Observable Challenge is retained and is NOT converted into a hard relational rejection gate (CAND-099 remains the reason). Relational research adds only four challenge questions: why A should condition B; why the relationship matters; what alternative explanation exists; what would falsify the relationship.

## 18. REGISTRY / ARTIFACT ARCHITECTURE

(Clause D24.) The minimum artifact architecture is: **(1) BASE REGISTRY** — new, begins EMPTY, entry only via Clause D2 validation; **(2) CONDITIONAL COMPONENT ADMISSION LEDGER** — research-stage admission records (Clause D10, Option C), kept separate from the System Assembly Candidate Register; **(3) RELATIONAL HYPOTHESIS REGISTRATION** — the existing framework's registration artifacts (SEED-002 precedent) with no new registry; **(4) INCREMENT EVIDENCE RECORDS** — results of assembled tests recorded in the evidence ledger, linked to their relational hypothesis, following the existing EVD record convention. No separate Conversion Registry is created initially. The System Assembly Candidate Register V1 is left **unchanged** (see Clause D10).

## 19. GOVERNANCE OWNERSHIP

(Clause D23.) Using existing governance terminology (owner authorization; governance/adjudication review artifacts; G1 V3 holistic adjudication):

| Action | Approving authority |
|---|---|
| Creation of a Base (registry entry) | Owner, on adjudication-review recommendation following a completed Clause D2 validation study |
| Admission of a Conditional Component | Governance review under Relational Framework V1 §4 conditional-admission rules |
| Admission of a Relational Hypothesis | Registration + owner authorization (SEED-002 precedent) |
| Level-2 composition | Owner (each Level-2 registered separately) |
| Any Level-3+ exception | Owner + separate governance milestone |
| Assembled economic qualification | G1 V3 holistic adjudication + owner |
| Reopening any closed research line | Owner only, exclusively via a new governed hypothesis satisfying Clause D13 |

## 20. STOP RULES

(Clause D25.) Qualitative stop rules — no arbitrary bps gates:
1. No validated Base exists → no assembled experiment may run.
2. No valid counterfactual can be constructed → no experiment.
3. A relationship batch becomes permutation-driven → the batch is halted and the family paused.
4. Repeated negative increment evidence within a relational family → the family is paused/closed on evidence (recorded as negative knowledge; base and component characterization evidence survive).
5. Component admission depends on post-hoc results → rejected, and the reviewing authority is notified.
6. Execution economics cannot be represented coherently under one cost model → stop.

# PART IV — FORMAL DOCTRINE TEXT

## 21. FORMAL PROPOSED DOCTRINE — D1–D25

Each clause states Requirement (R), Prohibition (P), Rationale (W), Governance consequence (G). This text is written to be ratifiable with minimal rewriting.

**D1 — Base legitimacy.**
R: A Base is a registered, deterministic decision process determining trade participation/execution under a declared scope, opportunity population, execution model, cost model, and predeclared evaluation protocol; registration is outcome-blind and forward-scoped. P: An indicator, state observation, historical subset, post-hoc favorable period, hypothetical P&L, result-derived combination, or carved component of a closed experiment shall not be a Base. W: Only a real decision process can carry an increment test without circularity. G: Objects not satisfying D1 cannot be proposed for validation.

**D2 — Base validation and viability.**
R: A Base enters the registry only through a new prospective Base-validation study: independent registration; forward-justified scope recorded before measurement; outcome-blind design; reproducibility; realistic costs; measured decision economics; confirmation separation where applicable. BASE VALIDATED ≠ QUALIFIED ALPHA. P: No retrospective subset of any historical program may be promoted to Base. W: Separation of validation from Alpha qualification permits decision contexts to exist without re-imposing the measured-dead standalone gate. G: Base viability — decision economics not deeply negative under the registered cost model — is adjudicated holistically by the owner on adjudication-review recommendation; no numeric cut-off.

**D3 — Conditional component.**
R: A conditional component is a deterministic, provenance-bearing observable/event/state admitted for a specific relational purpose; it survives the G0 validity discipline and MOC; it qualifies only by incremental value over a ratified Base. P: "Failed standalone, therefore conditional" is prohibited as a rationale; standalone failure neither qualifies nor disqualifies a component. W: Negative-standalone layers are the point of assembly research; mechanism, not failure, justifies conditional roles. G: Components admitted under Relational Framework V1 §4 rules.

**D4 — Relational hypothesis.**
R: A relational hypothesis declares components, temporal/structural relationship, mechanism rationale, expected dependency, expected economic consequence, counterfactual, and execution context; it is predeclared, falsifiable, and bounded. P: No admission on mutual historical interest alone; no post-outcome redesign. W: Mechanism-driven admission is the defense against permutation mining. G: Registration + owner authorization before measurement.

**D5 — Assembled decision as economic unit.**
R: The assembled decision — Base + predeclared component(s) + one execution model + one cost model — is the unit of economic qualification; the Base opportunity set is preserved as far as reasonably possible. P: Component-level P&L is never qualification evidence. W: Real systems are evaluated whole. G: Assembled tests are registered like experiments.

**D6 — Incremental economic qualification.**
R: Qualification requires incremental value of BASE + COMPONENT over BASE on the Base's population, outcome, and cost schedule; all metrics predeclared; default primary = the Base's own economic outcome; adjudication holistic. P: No post-outcome metric selection; no universal numeric threshold. W: The increment, not the component, is the claim. G: Adjudication under G1 V3 practice.

**D7 — Single cost/execution model.**
R: One end-to-end execution incurs one coherent cost treatment on the Base trade schedule. P: No independent component cost charging; no free filters; no component-P&L summation; no double counting; no hypothetical fills. W: Only end-to-end registered execution measures real economics. G: Cost-model integrity is a validity gate for assembled tests.

**D8 — No-rescue rule.**
R: The primary question of any relational experiment is whether conditional information improves a legitimate Base; SEED-002's rescue framing (CAND-083 × CAND-081) is the cited counterexample. P: "Can State A rescue State B?" and any question presupposing an economically invalid Base are invalid primary questions. W: Rescue framing produced the record's only negative relational result and invites selection games. G: Hypothesis admission rejects rescue-framed registrations.

**D9 — Base-registry firewall.**
R: The Base Registry begins EMPTY; entry only through Clause D2 prospective validation. P: No automatic promotion of CAND-077/081/083/099, H01 (including the contemporary cell), ORD, or any closed artifact; historical evidence motivates but never substitutes for prospective validation. W: An empty registry is the structural guarantee against retroactive selection. G: Registry entries are individually owner-approved.

**D10 — Conditional-component admission path.**
R: Conditional components are admitted through a **separate admission path** (Option C); the System Assembly Candidate Register V1 is left unchanged and retains its independent-qualification bar for production components. P: The Assembly Register's bar shall not be weakened to absorb a research-stage class. W: Production assembly and research-stage conditional evaluation answer different questions; merging them would corrupt both bars. G: A research-stage admission ledger records conditional-component admission; production entry later requires the Assembly Register's own bar.

**D11 — Relational depth.**
R: Level 1 (Base + one component) is the default qualification unit; Level 2 requires each component's established Level-1 increment plus an interaction mechanism rationale, under separate registration; Level 3+ prohibited absent separate governance. P: No unearned higher-depth testing. W: Depth multiplies degrees of freedom and attribution ambiguity. G: Depth is granted per milestone, never assumed.

**D12 — Permutation control.**
R: Pairings are mechanism-driven and predeclared; hypotheses are admitted in controlled predeclared batches reviewed per milestone; no new same-family batch without a distinct mechanism or new evidence. P: Exhaustive combination search, post-hoc component selection, permutation ranking, "best combination" search, threshold search within combinations, repeated retries. W: Batch discipline bounds multiple testing without an arbitrary cap. G: Batch admission is a governance review event.

**D13 — Closed-line firewall.**
R: Existing closed-line rules are retained verbatim; closed candidates contribute negative knowledge only; reference to a closed artifact requires a newly governed, independently justified hypothesis. P: Reintroduction of closed candidates as positive relational inputs; silent recycling. W: Closures are claims, not erasable history. G: Violations void the affected hypothesis.

**D14 — Protected-forward firewall.**
R: CAND-015, CAND-024, CAND-035 remain fully excluded from relational discovery and qualification. P: Performance inspection, relational use, indirect leakage. W: Forward integrity is non-negotiable. G: Permanent exclusion; no exception process.

**D15 — Temporal / hierarchical relationships.**
R: HTF→LTF-style stacks require deterministic definitions, timestamp ordering without look-ahead, synchronization rules, explicit maximum temporal distance, event dependency, counterfactual, one final execution decision, and one economic outcome; practitioner terminology is operationalized before admission. P: Nomenclature without operationalization; cross-timeframe look-ahead. W: Ordered decision stacks are only testable with strict temporal semantics. G: Each stack layer passes G0 discipline before stack registration.

**D16 — Liquidity-hypothesis compatibility.**
R: Future liquidity hypotheses (movement toward identifiable liquidity locations with sequential interaction) are hostable as Level-1/2 relational hypotheses over a ratified Base under full admission. P: Operationalizing liquidity thresholds, stop clusters, pending-order zones, or sweep rules under this doctrine's authority. W: Hosting capability is not endorsement. G: Any such hypothesis enters only through the normal relational admission process.

**D17 — Base vs conditional lifecycle.**
R: Objects progress — where evidence warrants — through characterization, admission, qualification, and production readiness; Base validation is a parallel path; not every object travels every stage. P: Conflating characterization with qualification, or admission with production readiness. W: Lifecycle clarity prevents status inflation. G: Status changes are recorded at each governed step.

**D18 — H01 treatment.**
R: H01 remains closed under its original economic adjudication; the contemporary-era cell is preserved as evidence and may motivate a future modern-era Base-validation study that is newly registered, forward-justified, scoped before outcomes, and outcome-blind. P: Treating the contemporary cell as a Base; presenting a future validation study as reopening H01. W: Prospective validation, not governance declaration, confers Base status. G: Any such study requires owner authorization as a new registration.

**D19 — Existing SRE treatment.**
R: CAND-077/081/083 historical evidence and lifecycle governance are unchanged; future reuse requires the relational admission path; SEED-002 stays negative knowledge. P: Automatic Base or component status; automatic SEED-002 retesting. W: Existing lifecycle decisions stand until a governed act changes them. G: SRE pool disposition remains the owner's (pending owner decision from V37A adjudication).

**D20 — G1 V3 compatibility.**
R: The G1 V3 pillars are preserved; relational qualification maps to G0 (integrity), G1 (assembled validity + economic evidence, 9 gates apply), further (holistic system-value); no universal hard thresholds. P: New gate layers or numeric gates. W: Smallest integration preserves the validated core. G: Relational adjudication follows existing holistic practice.

**D21 — Mechanism–Observable Challenge.**
R: The MOC is retained for all research, including relational; four relational questions are added (why A conditions B; why it matters; alternative explanations; falsification). P: Converting mechanism quality into an absolute discovery gate (CAND-099 lesson). W: Weak mechanisms can carry informative observables. G: MOC remains advisory review, not rejection.

**D22 — Anti-p-hacking doctrine.**
R: Prospective registration; no post-hoc Base creation; no historical favorable-subset promotion; no exhaustive combinations; predeclared pairings; mechanism rationale; depth limits; no threshold tuning; no post-outcome metric selection; no favorable-period selection; no repeated retries; closed-candidate firewall; protected-forward firewall; one execution/cost model; explicit counterfactual; no result-informed relationship redesign. P: Any deviation from the foregoing. W: These are the conditions under which relational evidence is interpretable. G: Deviations void the affected evidence.

**D23 — Governance ownership.**
R: Approvals follow Section 19's table using existing terminology. P: Self-authorization of Bases, components, hypotheses, depth exceptions, or line reopenings. W: Every new authority in this doctrine is an owner act. G: Unauthorized objects are void.

**D24 — Registry / artifact architecture.**
R: Maintain: Base Registry (empty until D2 entries); Conditional-Component Admission Ledger (research stage); relational hypothesis registrations (existing framework); increment evidence records (evidence ledger, EVD convention). P: Creating all five proposed registries; duplicating the Assembly Register. W: Minimum architecture prevents ambiguity without proliferation. G: Registries are implemented only after ratification, as a separate governed milestone.

**D25 — Doctrine-level stop rules.**
R: The stop rules of Section 20 apply at every stage boundary; no arbitrary bps gates. P: Continuing a relational family after repeated negative increment evidence; running without a validated Base. W: The doctrine closes on evidence, not enthusiasm. G: Stop events are recorded as negative knowledge and require owner decision to resume.
