# G0 CANDIDATE GENERATION

## 1. Research Factory V2 Context
The V7 candidate generation and subsequent G1 audit process revealed a critical insight via the failure of CAND-018: A repeatable behavioral event (like a pre-market liquidity vacuum) can have a positive median response but an unhedged, structurally negative mathematical expectancy due to a catastrophic left tail (macro trend days). The V8 cycle operates under the Economic-First directive to proactively address this by introducing **pre-entry state conditioning**.

## 2. V8 Research Question
> **Can we identify an objectively measurable PRE-ENTRY state that determines whether a recurring event is likely to produce a favorable post-entry response or an adverse regime?**

The goal is to discover events where the market itself provides observable information *before entry* about whether the setup is likely to succeed or fail, effectively isolating the favorable median behavior from the adverse left tail without relying on reactive stop-losses.

## 3. Candidate Generation Method
Candidates were generated by pairing known, repeatable market events (time-of-day, session boundaries) with an explicit pre-entry KPI designed to filter out the adverse regime. No data scanning, backtesting, or ML mining was utilized. All mechanics are deterministic and frozen at G0.

## 4. Mechanism Families
- **A — Liquidity / Auction:** Recurring liquidity events where pre-event volatility state determines continuation versus reversal.
- **B — Cross-Market Information:** A recurring market event where confirmation or disagreement in another market determines the response.
- **C — Volatility Transition:** A recurring volatility event where compression/expansion state determines whether subsequent movement persists.
- **D — Session Mechanics:** Recurring session transitions where prior directional state determines continuation versus reversal.

## 5. Candidates Considered

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-021
### Name: Pre-Auction Liquidity Vacuum Reversal Conditioned by Volatility Regime
### Mechanism Family: A — Liquidity / Auction
### Repeatable Event: 
US Equity Pre-Market Drift. Measured from 08:30:00 EST open to 09:30:00 EST open.
### Pre-Entry State/KPI: 
**D1 Volatility Regime.** Measured using the previous day's True Range (TR) compared to the previous day's D1 ATR(14). 
- *Favorable State:* Previous Day TR <= 1.5x D1 ATR(14) (Normal liquidity environment).
- *Adverse Regime:* Previous Day TR > 1.5x D1 ATR(14) (Macro shock / structural repricing environment).
### Expected Post-Event Direction: 
Reversal (against the 08:30-09:30 drift).
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at 09:30:00 EST open (if drift > 1.5x M5 ATR(14) and State is Favorable).
### Deterministic Exit: 
10:30:00 EST open (fixed 60-minute horizon).
### Opportunity Integrity: 
Strictly one event per day.
### Economic Plausibility: 
By explicitly filtering out the macro-shock days (the cause of CAND-018's left tail) before entry, the strategy attempts to isolate the structurally positive median of the liquidity vacuum fill, converting it into a positive mathematical expectancy.
### Failure Mode: 
Fails if the previous day's volatility is uncorrelated with today's 09:30 macro momentum continuation, leaving the left tail intact.
### Data Required: 
USATECHIDXUSD (M5 and D1).
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Single instrument.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Independent from CAND-018 because it replaces the SMA directional filter with an explicit D1 Volatility Regime filter designed to predict the left-tail condition.
### G0 Decision: PROMOTE TO G1

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-022
### Name: European Close Fixing Reversal Conditioned by Cross-Market Divergence
### Mechanism Family: B — Cross-Market Information
### Repeatable Event: 
The European Close / London Fixing window drift (08:00 EST to 11:30 EST).
### Pre-Entry State/KPI: 
**Cross-Market Divergence.** The 08:00-11:30 drift magnitude in EURUSD compared to GBPUSD.
- *Favorable State (Fixing Flow):* EURUSD drift > 1.0 D1 ATR, while GBPUSD drift < 0.5 D1 ATR. (The move is isolated to EUR, implying temporary institutional fixing flows).
- *Adverse Regime (Macro Trend):* Both EURUSD and GBPUSD drift > 1.0 D1 ATR. (The move is a broad USD macro trend, implying continuation).
### Expected Post-Event Direction: 
Reversal of the 08:00-11:30 EURUSD drift.
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at 11:30:00 EST open in EURUSD.
### Deterministic Exit: 
13:30:00 EST open (fixed 2-hour horizon).
### Opportunity Integrity: 
Maximum one event per day.
### Economic Plausibility: 
Fixing flows create temporary supply/demand imbalances that revert once the fix is complete. Cross-market divergence provides an objective mechanism to separate these temporary flows from genuine macroeconomic shifts.
### Failure Mode: 
Fails if GBPUSD is independently driven by UK-specific news during the window, creating a false divergence signal that leads to trading against a genuine EUR macro trend.
### Data Required: 
EURUSD (M5/H1/D1) and GBPUSD (M5/H1/D1).
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Dual instrument correlation.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Wholly independent mechanism family utilizing cross-asset divergence.
### G0 Decision: PROMOTE TO G1

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-023
### Name: London Open Momentum Conditioned by Asian Session Compression
### Mechanism Family: C — Volatility Transition
### Repeatable Event: 
London Open Initial Momentum (03:00 EST to 04:00 EST).
### Pre-Entry State/KPI: 
**Asian Session Volatility Compression.** The High-Low range from 20:00 EST to 03:00 EST compared to the D1 ATR(14).
- *Favorable State (Accumulation):* Asian Range < 0.5x D1 ATR(14). The market has stored energy.
- *Adverse Regime (Distribution):* Asian Range > 1.0x D1 ATR(14). The market is already expanded and prone to mean-reversion whipsaws.
### Expected Post-Event Direction: 
Continuation of the 03:00-04:00 drift direction.
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at 04:00:00 EST open.
### Deterministic Exit: 
08:00:00 EST open (before the NY open volatility).
### Opportunity Integrity: 
Maximum one event per day.
### Economic Plausibility: 
Volatility is cyclical. Extreme compression during the Asian session reliably precedes expansion. By waiting for the first hour of London to dictate the directional bias, the strategy captures the ensuing expansion phase while filtering out whipsaw environments.
### Failure Mode: 
Fails if the 03:00-04:00 move is routinely a "fake-out" liquidity sweep rather than genuine directional initiation.
### Data Required: 
XAUUSD or EURUSD (M5/H1/D1).
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Single instrument.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
New mechanism explicitly formalizing the SMC "accumulation/expansion" concept into an objective volatility-transition KPI.
### G0 Decision: PROMOTE TO G1

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-024
### Name: Friday De-Risking Conditioned by Morning Exhaustion
### Mechanism Family: D — Session Mechanics
### Repeatable Event: 
Friday afternoon session (12:00 EST).
### Pre-Entry State/KPI: 
**Morning Exhaustion State.** The Friday morning price action (08:00 EST open to 12:00 EST open) compared to the weekly high.
- *Favorable State:* The High between 08:00 and 12:00 exceeds the Weekly High (prior to Friday), BUT the 12:00 open price is LOWER than the 08:00 open price. (A failed breakout / exhaustion).
- *Adverse Regime:* The 12:00 open is higher than the 08:00 open. (A persistent trend).
### Expected Post-Event Direction: 
Short (Continuation of the exhaustion down to the weekly close).
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at 12:00:00 EST open.
### Deterministic Exit: 
15:45:00 EST open.
### Opportunity Integrity: 
Maximum one event per week.
### Economic Plausibility: 
Positional de-risking is a real phenomenon, but it only overrides normal market flow when buyers are explicitly exhausted. The "failed weekly breakout" condition formalizes this exhaustion, providing the necessary catalyst for the afternoon liquidation.
### Failure Mode: 
Fails due to very low opportunity frequency (CAND-020 vulnerability) or if algorithmic buying simply buys the dip regardless of the morning failure.
### Data Required: 
USATECHIDXUSD (M5/H1/D1).
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Single instrument.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Direct iteration on the failed CAND-020, replacing a vague "strong week" condition with a strict, localized "morning exhaustion" KPI.
### G0 Decision: PROMOTE TO G1
