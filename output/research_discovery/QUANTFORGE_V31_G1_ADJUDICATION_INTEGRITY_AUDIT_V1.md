## 8. Mean vs Median vs Win Rate

The G1 V3 framework states:

> "Mean net: Primary economic measurement — central tendency"
> "Median net: Distributional evidence — typical event outcome"

When mean and median disagree:
- The mean is the primary measure
- The median provides distributional context
- Neither automatically dominates, but the primary measure carries more weight in adjudication

For both CAND-092 and CAND-093, the mean contradicts the hypothesis while the median supports it. The primary measure (mean) prevailing is consistent with the framework when absolute economics are also negative.
