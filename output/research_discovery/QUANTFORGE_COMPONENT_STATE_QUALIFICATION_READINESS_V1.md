## 13. Rare-Event Validation Doctrine
Rare events are validated by sufficient independent event occurrences, not by arbitrary short calendar windows. A 4-event/year mechanism cannot be judged by a 7-day paper observation. A Rare-Event Forward Qualification must be **OPEN-ENDED / EVENT-DEPENDENT**, establishing a minimum target operational observation count rather than a target date.

State information is not a trading strategy. A state artifact only begins formal qualification when it is attached to a specific, previously qualified Alpha to measure the delta in that Alpha's performance.
