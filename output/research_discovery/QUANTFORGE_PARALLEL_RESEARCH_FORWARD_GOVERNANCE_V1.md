# QuantForge Parallel Research & Forward Governance V1

## 1. Purpose
Establish the new operating model separating active forward observation from new research generation. This explicitly parallelizes the QuantForge workflow to prevent the running CAND-015 forward observation from blocking new candidate discovery.

## 2. Track A — CAND-015 Forward Validation
**Status: ACTIVE / PROTECTED**
The purpose of Track A is to determine whether the frozen, economically viable CAND-015 object survives real-time market observation and realistic paper execution. The planned current unattended session is: CAND-015 7-DAY FORWARD PAPER OBSERVATION.

## 3. Track B — Research Discovery
**Status: ACTIVE / G0**
The Research Factory V2 is not paused while CAND-015 forward observation runs. New research discovery proceeds independently to discover new independently justified tradeable behavioral mechanisms.

## 4. Independence Rules
The two tracks are operationally independent. New research must not modify, tune, reinterpret, or otherwise influence the CAND-015 forward observation. Likewise, partial CAND-015 forward results must not be used to select, filter, or formulate new candidates. This creates two independent evidence streams.

## 5. Partial-Observation Freeze
CAND-015 forward observation is a PROTECTED VALIDATION TRACK. During the observation:
- no strategy changes;
- no parameter changes;
- no threshold changes;
- no entry/exit changes;
- no filtering;
- no execution-cost changes;
- no interpretation-driven modifications.

The running observation must remain untouched until its complete log is available. No scientific or economic conclusion may be drawn from the partial 7-day forward observation before the session is complete and independently audited. A zero-event or low-event partial observation is NOT a failure. A positive paper result in a partial observation is NOT a promotion. The forward log must first reach the declared observation endpoint.

## 6. G0 Research Restart
The next research cycle must start from **G0 — Candidate Generation**. Do not jump directly to G1, G2, scientific validation, tick data, Parquet, Stage 1, Stage 2, or bot construction. The Research Factory V2 sequence remains G0 → G1 → G2 → G3 → G4 → G5 → G6 with evidence-earned resource escalation.

Future candidates must remain mechanism-diverse (cross-market information flow, volatility-state transitions, liquidity/auction behavior, price/time structure, order-flow/microstructure where data supports it, session effects, structural market behavior, other objectively formalizable mechanisms). Do not create multiple superficial variations of CAND-015.

A methodological lesson from CAND-015: A promising discovery can emerge from the structure:
REPEATABLE EVENT → PRE-ENTRY MARKET STATE / KPI → CONDITIONAL POST-EVENT RESPONSE → EXECUTABLE CAPTURE.
This is now an important candidate-generation pattern. But explicitly add: CAND-015 is NOT evidence that its particular mechanism family is universally superior.

The G0 doctrine is strengthened so candidates should preferentially answer:
- What event repeatedly occurs?
- What direction does price tend to move after that event?
- Under what observable pre-entry state/KPI does that conditional response strengthen or weaken?
- Where is the executable entry?
- What deterministic post-entry response is being measured?
This is a candidate-generation lens, NOT a mandatory strategy template. Do not require all future candidates to resemble CAND-015.

## 7. Resource Escalation
The running CAND-015 forward observation does NOT authorize expensive infrastructure for unrelated research. New candidates still must earn G1 → G2 → deeper infrastructure. Do not resume the historical ORD/Parquet pipeline merely because it exists.

## 8. CAND-015 Completion Criteria
The forward track cannot be adjudicated until:
- the declared 7-day observation completes;
- session artifacts are preserved;
- MT5 health is audited;
- event count is established;
- paper trades are reconciled;
- execution quote differences are analyzed;
- reconnects/data gaps are reviewed;
- frozen identity is verified.

After completion, separate forward-validation adjudication is required. There is no automatic promotion.

## 9. Governance Integrity
This governance is active as of 2026-08-26. No automatic promotion, no mid-session changes, no resource misuse.
