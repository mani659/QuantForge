## 6. Candidates Considered

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-028
### Name: Volume Impact Exhaustion Reversal
### Mechanism: Liquidity / Impact
A massive volume spike on a short timeframe (e.g., M1) that closes poorly (long wick or narrow spread) signals institutional absorption of retail panic/FOMO. The liquidity event exhausts the near-term directional impact, causing an immediate reversal.
### Repeatable Event: 
M1 volume is > 5x the 20-period moving average of volume, AND the close of the candle is within the lower 25% of the candle range (for an up-candle) or upper 25% (for a down-candle).
### Information State: 
The market has absorbed a massive liquidity shock without yielding directional ground. Directional conviction is exhausted.
### Predicted Direction: 
Reversal (counter to the volume spike direction).
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at the exact open of the subsequent M1 candle.
### Deterministic Exit: 
Fixed horizon: Close at the exact open of the 16th M1 candle (15-minute hold).
### Opportunity Integrity: 
- **Onset:** Close of the qualifying M1 volume spike candle.
- **Completion:** Close of the 15-minute holding period.
- **Duplicate Suppression:** Ignore any new volume spikes during an active hold.
- **Re-arm:** Immediately upon completion of the hold.
### Economic Plausibility: 
High per-event expectancy expected, as these are typically blow-off tops/bottoms with immediate mean reversion. The expected gross move should comfortably clear standard M1 friction for highly liquid instruments.
### Expected Frequency: 
Low/Medium (Event Opportunist).
### Intended Component Role: 
Event Opportunist (captures rare, high-volatility structural exhaustion).
### Data Required: 
M1 price and volume.
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Crypto (BTCUSD), FX (EURUSD), Indices (USATECHIDXUSD).
### Failure Mode: 
If the post-spike 15-minute drift is statistically indistinguishable from zero or continues in the spike direction, the institutional absorption hypothesis is falsified.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Independent of DISC-021 (not a volatility regime filter) and DISC-025 (not an Asian sweep). This relies purely on isolated volume/price-action anomalies.
### G0 Decision: 
**PROMOTE**

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-029
### Name: Rates/FX Lead-Lag Dispersion
### Mechanism: Relative-Value / Dispersion
US Treasury yields typically drive USD strength. If yields spike (rates rise) but the USD fails to follow during the same window, it indicates an underlying structural USD weakness or localized supply/demand mismatch that will resolve into a USD sell-off once the rates shock dissipates.
### Repeatable Event: 
Over a rolling 30-minute window, the US 10-Year Treasury Yield (or TLT ETF proxy) makes a > 1.5 standard deviation directional move, but USDJPY makes a move of less than 0.5 standard deviations in the correlated direction (or moves inversely).
### Information State: 
Macro correlations are momentarily broken, revealing relative structural weakness in the lagging asset.
### Predicted Direction: 
Continuation of the divergence (i.e., short USDJPY if yields rose but USDJPY failed to rise).
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at the exact close of the 30-minute divergence window.
### Deterministic Exit: 
Fixed horizon: Close at 16:00 ET (US Session Close).
### Opportunity Integrity: 
- **Onset:** Close of the 30-minute divergence window.
- **Completion:** US Session Close.
- **Duplicate Suppression:** Only one active trade per day.
- **Re-arm:** Next trading day.
### Economic Plausibility: 
High expectancy. Macro divergence trades typically yield significant intraday trends.
### Expected Frequency: 
Low.
### Intended Component Role: 
Cross-Market Component / Regime Specialist.
### Data Required: 
M1/M5 price for USDJPY and TLT/US10Y.
### Cross-Market Scope: 
FX vs Rates.
### Failure Mode: 
If USDJPY catches up to the yield move rather than continuing to diverge, the hypothesis is falsified.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Independent. No prior QuantForge candidate has utilized rates/FX dispersion as a direct structural trigger.
### G0 Decision: 
**PROMOTE**

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-030
### Name: Asian Session Value Area Rejection
### Mechanism: Structural Price Acceptance
The Asian session (low liquidity) creates a tightly defined value area. When the European open tests the outer boundary of this Asian distribution and immediately rejects (closes back inside), it signals structural acceptance of the Asian price distribution and a lack of European directional conviction.
### Repeatable Event: 
Between 08:00 and 09:00 London time, price breaks outside the Asian session (00:00 - 08:00) high/low but the M15 candle closes back inside the Asian range.
### Information State: 
European liquidity failed to establish a new price extreme and fell back into the established Asian distribution.
### Predicted Direction: 
Mean reversion toward the Asian session median price.
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at the exact open of the subsequent M15 candle.
### Deterministic Exit: 
Limit target at the Asian session median price, OR fixed time stop at 16:30 London time.
### Opportunity Integrity: 
- **Onset:** Close of the M15 rejection candle.
- **Completion:** Target hit or 16:30 time boundary.
- **Duplicate Suppression:** One trade per boundary per day.
- **Re-arm:** Next trading day.
### Economic Plausibility: 
Medium. The gross move (from boundary to median) must be large enough to clear friction.
### Expected Frequency: 
Medium.
### Intended Component Role: 
Session Component.
### Data Required: 
M15 price.
### Cross-Market Scope: 
FX (EURUSD, GBPUSD).
### Failure Mode: 
If price typically breaks through the opposite boundary or fails to revert to the median, the value-area acceptance hypothesis is falsified.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Independent of CAND-023 (London Momentum) and DISC-025 (Asian Sweep). This explicitly requires a failed breakout and reversion, not momentum or a stop-run.
### G0 Decision: 
**PROMOTE**

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-031
### Name: Volatility/Price Decoupling
### Mechanism: Cross-Market Price Discovery
The SP500 and VIX usually move inversely. When the SP500 makes a new intraday low but the VIX fails to make a new intraday high (VIX non-confirmation), it signals that downside price action is exhausted and lacks panic/volatility expansion, predicting an immediate market bounce.
### Repeatable Event: 
The SP500 prints a new trailing 2-hour low on an M5 candle, but the corresponding VIX M5 high is strictly lower than the VIX high from the previous SP500 low.
### Information State: 
Downside price action is no longer supported by expanding implied volatility.
### Predicted Direction: 
Reversal (Long SP500).
### Executable Entry: 
Market order at the exact open of the subsequent M5 candle.
### Deterministic Exit: 
Fixed horizon: 60 minutes.
### Opportunity Integrity: 
- **Onset:** Close of the M5 non-confirmation candle.
- **Completion:** Close of the 60-minute holding period.
- **Duplicate Suppression:** Block new entries during hold.
- **Re-arm:** Immediately upon completion.
### Economic Plausibility: 
High. SP500 reversals driven by volatility exhaustion often yield strong intraday bounces.
### Expected Frequency: 
Low.
### Intended Component Role: 
Diversifier (Crash-absorber / Reversal).
### Data Required: 
M5 SP500 and VIX.
### Cross-Market Scope: 
Equity Indices.
### Failure Mode: 
If the SP500 continues its downtrend despite VIX non-confirmation, the decoupling hypothesis is falsified.
### Closed-Line Independence: 
Independent of H01 (DISC-023), which focused on volatility *response* to shocks, not structural decoupling during intraday trends.
### G0 Decision: 
**PROMOTE**
