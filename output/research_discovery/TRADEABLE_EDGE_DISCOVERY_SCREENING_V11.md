## 2. V11 Research Thesis
Previous G0/G1 cycles (e.g., CAND-018, CAND-030) demonstrated that high win rates and positive median returns often mask a severe negative expectancy caused by left-tail adverse excursions. ## 3. Candidate Generation Method
Candidates were generated by exploring structural events that force persistent institutional action (e.g., session breakouts, scheduled fixing flows, cross-market confirmations). Designs relying purely on "reversals" or "bounces" (which often generate left tails) were deliberately avoided in favor of momentum, continuation, and forced-flow mechanics.

## 4. Mechanism Families
- **FAMILY B (Breakout / Price-Discovery Continuation):** A structural session breach forces participants to reprice and reposition.
- **FAMILY C (Forced Flow / Inventory):** Scheduled benchmark execution forces institutional liquidity demands.
- **FAMILY D (Cross-Market Repricing):** Simultaneous macro confirmations validate true momentum, filtering out false idiosyncratic shocks.

### Candidate ID: CAND-G0-032
### Name: NY Open Structural Momentum Ignition
### Mechanism Family: FAMILY B — BREAKOUT / PRICE-DISCOVERY CONTINUATION
### Mechanistic Explanation:
When the New York session opens, equity indices experience peak daily liquidity. If the initial 30 minutes of the NY session completely breaches the entire London session range, it signals profound institutional directional alignment. This structural breakout forces offsides European/Asian participants to cover and invites aggressive trend-following algorithmic flow, creating persistent directional volume for the remainder of the US session.
### Repeatable Event:
At exactly 10:00 ET, the 30-minute NY initial balance (09:30–10:00 ET) closes strictly above the London session high (03:00–09:30 ET) or strictly below the London session low.
### Information State:
The market has decisively broken a multi-hour value area during peak liquidity, establishing a new accepted price extreme.
### Predicted Direction:
Continuation of the breakout direction.
### Executable Entry:
Market order at exactly 10:00 ET.
### Deterministic Exit:
Time stop at 16:00 ET (US Cash Close).
### Opportunity Integrity:
- **Event onset:** 10:00 ET.
- **Event completion:** 16:00 ET.
- **Duplicate suppression:** Maximum one entry per day.
- **Re-arm:** Next trading day.
### Positive-Expectancy Thesis:
Breakouts backed by peak session liquidity often result in intraday "trend days." The continuation magnitude is fundamentally uncapped within the session, naturally generating the asymmetric right-tail winners required for positive expectancy.
### Adverse-Risk Structure:
False breakouts (fake-outs) will revert and chop around the entry price. However, the time stop at 16:00 ET forces an exit before overnight macro risk or gap risk can occur, strictly bounding the maximum adverse excursion to a single session's drift.
### Economic Plausibility:
The magnitude of an average NY session trend day comfortably exceeds standard index friction (e.g., 2.0 points for USATECHIDXUSD).
### Expected Frequency:
Low to Medium.
### Intended Component Role:
Session Component.
### Data Required:
M1 USATECHIDXUSD.
### Cross-Market Scope:
Equity Indices (SP500, NASDAQ, DJIA).
### Failure Mode:
If NY breakouts systematically revert to the mean within the session, the trend-day momentum hypothesis is falsified.
### Closed-Line Independence:
Independent of all previous candidates (does not rely on Friday mechanics, reversals, or simple volatility filters).
### G0 Decision:

Between 10:00 ET and 10:45 ET, EURUSD moves directionally by > 0.25x D1 ATR, establishing the dominant fixing flow trajectory.
### Information State:
A forced-liquidity imbalance is actively driving price toward a scheduled execution benchmark.
### Predicted Direction:
Continuation of the trajectory into the exact time of the fix.
### Executable Entry:
Market order at exactly 10:45 ET.
### Deterministic Exit:

- **Event onset:** 10:45 ET.
- **Event completion:** 11:00 ET.

The forced nature of fixing execution creates a persistent, one-way liquidity vacuum into the deadline. The asymmetric payoff comes from the accelerating price impact, capturing a dense gross move in an extremely short window.

### Mechanism Family: FAMILY D — CROSS-MARKET REPRICING
### Mechanistic Explanation:
Precious metals (Gold/Silver) generally share macro drivers. An idiosyncratic liquidity void or stop-run often affects only one asset and quickly reverts. However, if XAUUSD and XAGUSD simultaneously experience a profound momentum shock in the same direction, it validates a genuine macro repricing event, signaling high likelihood of persistent continuation.
### Repeatable Event:
Over a rolling 15-minute window, both XAUUSD and XAGUSD simultaneously register a return > 0.5x their respective D1 ATR, in the same direction.
### Information State:
A macro momentum shock is confirmed across correlated assets, filtering out false/isolated spikes.
### Predicted Direction:
Continuation of the momentum direction.
### Executable Entry:
Market order on XAUUSD at the exact close of the confirming 15-minute window.
### Deterministic Exit:
Time stop exactly 2 hours after entry.
### Opportunity Integrity:
- **Event onset:** Close of the confirming 15-minute window.
- **Event completion:** 2 hours post-entry.
- **Duplicate suppression:** Block new signals during active hold.
- **Re-arm:** Immediately after exit.
### Positive-Expectancy Thesis:
Cross-confirmed momentum filters out false breakouts (the primary cause of left-tail losses in momentum strategies). The confirmed macro trend provides a persistent right-tail payoff.
### Adverse-Risk Structure:
False cross-market ignitions usually fizzle into low-volatility consolidation rather than violent V-shape reversals, naturally limiting the magnitude of losses before the 2-hour time exit.
### Economic Plausibility:
Macro repricing in gold over a 2-hour horizon routinely produces moves orders of magnitude larger than standard friction.
### Expected Frequency:
Low (Event Opportunist).
### Intended Component Role:
Cross-Market Component / Event Opportunist.
### Data Required:
M1 XAUUSD and M1 XAGUSD.
### Cross-Market Scope:
Correlated asset classes (e.g., SP500/NASDAQ).
### Failure Mode:

### Closed-Line Independence:
Independent of all previous candidates. (CAND-015 used leader-lag prediction; this uses simultaneous cross-asset confirmation to validate momentum).
