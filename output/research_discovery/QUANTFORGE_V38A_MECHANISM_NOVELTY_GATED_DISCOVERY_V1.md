### MECH-X01: BREAKOUT VELOCITY CONVICTION

**Mechanism ID:** MECH-X01

**Mechanism name:** Breakout Velocity Conviction

#### Mechanism statement

When price breaks a structural level, the SPEED at which it moves through the level reveals participant conviction. A rapid break (price traverses the level zone in few bars with large directional moves) indicates strong conviction from one participant class — the side pushing through the level has sufficient order flow to overwhelm the other side quickly. A slow, grinding break (price traverses the level zone over many bars with small moves and frequent reversals) indicates contested positioning — both sides are active and neither has clear conviction. The speed of penetration is an observable manifestation of the balance of conviction between participants at the structural level.

#### Participant hypothesis

- **Conviction-driven participants:** Large directional orders that push price through the level quickly. Their conviction is revealed by the speed of the move.
- **Opposition participants:** Resting orders at the level that resist the break. Their conviction is revealed by the slowing of the move.
- **Speed as conviction signal:** Rapid penetration = conviction participants overwhelmed opposition. Slow penetration = opposition is strong and the break may fail.

#### Observable

At M1 resolution:
- Bar E: price breaks a structural level (close beyond level)
- Bars E+1 through E+V: price traverses the "level zone" (the region around the structural level)
- V = number of bars from breakout to when price is clearly beyond the zone (e.g., close is >X bps beyond level)
- **Rapid break:** V is small (few bars to penetrate)
- **Slow break:** V is large (many bars to penetrate)

The observable is deterministic: count bars from breakout to clear penetration.

#### Distinguishing observable

The mechanism is distinguished from simple breakout validation by measuring HOW FAST price moves through the level zone, not WHETHER it holds beyond the level. Breakout validation asks "did price stay beyond the level?" Breakout velocity asks "how quickly did price get beyond the level?"

#### State/event transition

The transition is from "level contested" to "level penetrated with conviction" (rapid) or "level penetrated without conviction" (slow). The speed of the transition is the observable that reveals participant conviction.

#### Economic pathway

Rapid penetration → strong conviction from one side → continuation likely → follow the breakout direction.
Slow penetration → contested positioning → breakout may fail → avoid or fade.

The economic consequence follows from the conviction signal embedded in the speed of penetration.

#### Falsification

The mechanism is falsified if:
- Breakout velocity (V) does not predict subsequent directional continuation
- Rapid breaks and slow breaks show identical subsequent economics
- The speed of penetration is random relative to subsequent outcomes

#### Data requirements

- M1 OHLCV for a single instrument (e.g., USATECHIDXUSD)
- Structural level definition (frozen at registration)
- Level zone definition (frozen at registration)
- V measurement window (frozen at registration)

#### Execution feasibility

The information is observable in real time. After a breakout is detected, the velocity of penetration can be measured as subsequent bars complete. Entry after velocity is assessed, exit at session close.

#### Nearest prior mechanisms

- BASE-001 (structural level validation): validation measures WHETHER price holds; velocity measures HOW FAST it moves through. Different observable, different question.
- CAND-081 (structural level failure trap): CAND-081 measures whether the break fails within a window; velocity measures the speed of the break itself. Different observable.
- Post-magnitude directional drift (exhausted): drift measures the SIZE of the move; velocity measures the SPEED. Size and speed are different properties.

### MECH-X02: POST-EXTREME DIRECTIONAL ABSORPTION

**Mechanism ID:** MECH-X02

**Mechanism name:** Post-Extreme Directional Absorption

#### Mechanism statement

When price reaches a session extreme (session high or low), the first subsequent price movement away from the extreme reveals the conviction of participants defending that extreme. If price moves away from the extreme QUICKLY and DECISIVELY (large bars in the reversal direction), the extreme is being defended by participants with strong conviction — the defense is absorbing the probe. If price moves away from the extreme SLOWLY and INDECISIVELY (small bars, frequent reversals), the defense is weak and the extreme may be retested or broken. The quality of the initial response at the extreme is an observable manifestation of the conviction of the defending participant class.

#### Participant hypothesis

- **Extreme defenders:** Participants with positions or orders at the session extreme. When price reaches their level, they act to defend it (buying at session lows, selling at session highs).
- **Probe initiators:** Participants who pushed price to the extreme. Their conviction is revealed by whether they continue pushing or withdraw.
- **Absorption quality:** The speed and decisiveness of the reversal away from the extreme reveals the conviction of the defenders. Strong defense = quick, decisive reversal. Weak defense = slow, grinding reversal.

#### Observable

At M1 resolution:
- Bar E: price reaches a session extreme (new session high or low)
- Bars E+1 through E+A: price moves away from the extreme
- **Strong absorption:** Bars E+1 through E+A are large, directional, and close in the reversal direction
- **Weak absorption:** Bars E+1 through E+A are small, mixed, and close near the extreme

The observable is deterministic: measure the size, direction, and close location of bars immediately following the session extreme.

#### Distinguishing observable

The mechanism is distinguished from structural level research by focusing on SESSION EXTREMES (which reset daily) rather than structural levels (which persist across sessions). It is distinguished from mean reversion by measuring the QUALITY of the initial response (speed, decisiveness) rather than the DISTANCE from the extreme.

#### State/event transition

The transition is from "extreme probed" to "extreme defended" or "extreme breached." The quality of the initial response reveals which transition is occurring.

#### Economic pathway

Strong absorption at extreme → defenders have conviction → extreme holds → fade the probe direction.
Weak absorption at extreme → defenders lack conviction → extreme may break → avoid or follow the probe direction.

#### Falsification

The mechanism is falsified if:
- Absorption quality (speed/decisiveness of initial response) does not predict whether the extreme holds
- Strong and weak absorption show identical subsequent economics
- The quality of the initial response is random relative to subsequent outcomes

#### Data requirements

- M1 OHLCV for a single instrument (e.g., USATECHIDXUSD)
- Session extreme definition (highest high / lowest low of session so far)
- Absorption measurement window (A bars after extreme)

#### Execution feasibility

The information is observable in real time. After a session extreme is detected, the quality of the initial response can be measured as subsequent bars complete. Entry after absorption is assessed, exit at session close.

#### Nearest prior mechanisms

- CAND-081 (structural level failure trap): CAND-081 measures whether a structural level break fails within a window. X02 measures the quality of the response at a session extreme. Different trigger (session extreme vs. structural level break), different observable (response quality vs. failure timing).
- Mean reversion (exhausted): mean reversion fades displacement toward a mean. X02 measures the quality of the defense at an extreme. Different mechanism (defense quality vs. price deviation).
- Session-range compression (exhausted): compression measures the intraday range. X02 measures the response at the range boundary. Different concept.

### MECH-X03: DIRECTIONAL CONVICTION DECAY

**Mechanism ID:** MECH-X03

**Mechanism name:** Directional Conviction Decay

#### Mechanism statement

During a sustained directional trend, the INTERNAL QUALITY of successive directional bars reveals whether participant conviction is strengthening or weakening. A strong directional bar (large range, close near the extreme in the trend direction) indicates high conviction. A weak directional bar (small range, close far from the extreme, or close opposing the trend) indicates low conviction. The SEQUENCE of bar qualities across a trend reveals whether conviction is DECAYING (each successive bar is weaker) or SUSTAINING (each successive bar is strong). Conviction decay predicts trend exhaustion; conviction sustain predicts trend continuation. The decay process is the mechanism — it is not "momentum exhaustion" or "trend length" but specifically the degradation of per-bar conviction quality.

#### Participant hypothesis

- **Trend initiators:** Participants who started the trend. Their conviction is revealed by the quality of the early bars.
- **Trend followers:** Participants who join the trend. Their conviction is revealed by the quality of middle bars.
- **Late entrants:** Participants who join late. Their conviction is revealed by the quality of late bars.
- **Conviction decay:** As the trend progresses, each successive participant class has weaker conviction than the previous. This is revealed by degrading bar quality. The decay is a structural feature of trend development, not a statistical artifact.

#### Observable

At M1 resolution:
- A directional trend is detected (e.g., N consecutive bars in the same direction, or cumulative return exceeds threshold)
- For each bar in the trend, measure bar quality: range relative to recent average, close location relative to bar range (close near high in uptrend = high quality)
- **Conviction decay:** Bar quality decreases across the trend (each successive bar is weaker)
- **Conviction sustain:** Bar quality remains constant or increases across the trend

The observable is deterministic: compute a bar-quality metric for each bar in the trend and measure the trend of the quality metric.

#### Distinguishing observable

The mechanism is distinguished from MECH-N03 (trend quality) by measuring the DECAY PROCESS across a trend, not a single quality classification at one point. MECH-N03 classifies a trend as "impulse" or "grinding" at one moment. X03 tracks how conviction quality EVOLVES across the trend. The decay process is a dynamic, not a static classification.

The mechanism is distinguished from directional momentum/exhaustion (exhausted) by measuring per-bar conviction quality, not trend length or directional persistence. Momentum exhaustion asks "has the trend been going on long enough?" X03 asks "is each successive bar showing weaker conviction?"

#### State/event transition

The transition is from "conviction sustaining" to "conviction decaying." This occurs when the bar-quality trend turns negative (each successive bar is weaker). The transition is observable as a change in the quality trajectory.

#### Economic pathway

Conviction sustain → trend has structural backing → continuation likely → follow the trend.
Conviction decay → trend is losing structural backing → exhaustion likely → avoid or fade.

#### Falsification

The mechanism is falsified if:
- Conviction decay (degrading bar quality) does not predict trend exhaustion
- Trends with decaying conviction and sustaining conviction show identical subsequent economics
- Bar quality trajectory is random relative to subsequent outcomes

#### Data requirements

- M1 OHLCV for a single instrument (e.g., USATECHIDXUSD)
- Trend detection criteria (frozen at registration)
- Bar quality metric (frozen at registration)
- Decay measurement window (frozen at registration)

#### Execution feasibility

The information is observable in real time. After a trend is detected, the bar-quality trajectory can be measured as subsequent bars complete. Entry after decay is assessed, exit at session close.

#### Nearest prior mechanisms

- MECH-N03 (session-sequential trend quality): N03 classifies trend quality at one point (impulse vs. grinding). X03 tracks conviction decay across the trend. Different observable (single classification vs. trajectory), different mechanism (quality classification vs. decay process).
- Directional momentum / exhaustion (exhausted): momentum measures trend direction and length. X03 measures per-bar conviction quality. Different observable (direction vs. quality), different mechanism (momentum vs. conviction decay).
- Directional run-length persistence (exhausted): run-length measures how long the trend lasts. X03 measures how conviction degrades during the trend. Different concept.

### MECH-X04: INFORMATION ABSORPTION ASYMMETRY

**Mechanism ID:** MECH-X04

**Mechanism name:** Information Absorption Asymmetry

#### Mechanism statement

When a large price move occurs, the market's subsequent behavior reveals whether the move was driven by INFORMATIVE flow (information that justifies a permanent price change) or by POSITIONING flow (forced adjustments, stop-outs, or temporary imbalances that do not justify a permanent change). The distinguishing observable is the ASYMMETRY of the post-move price path: if price continues in the move direction with conviction (strong bars, close near extremes), the move was informative and the market is absorbing genuine information. If price retraces quickly with weak conviction in the opposite direction (small bars, mixed closes), the move was positioning-driven and the market is reversing the temporary imbalance. The asymmetry of the post-move path reveals the information content of the original move.

#### Participant hypothesis

- **Information-driven participants:** Trade on genuine information (earnings, macro data, structural shifts). Their flow creates moves that persist because the information is permanent.
- **Positioning-driven participants:** Trade due to forced adjustments (stop-outs, margin calls, rebalancing). Their flow creates moves that reverse because the underlying information has not changed.
- **Absorption asymmetry:** The post-move price path reveals which participant class dominated the original move. Asymmetric continuation (strong follow-through) = information. Symmetric reversion (quick retracement) = positioning.

#### Observable

At M1 resolution:
- Bar E: large directional move (return exceeds threshold S)
- Bars E+1 through E+M: post-move price path
- **Information absorption:** Bars E+1 through E+M continue in the move direction with conviction (strong bars, close near extremes)
- **Positioning reversal:** Bars E+1 through E+M retrace with weak conviction (small bars, mixed closes)

The observable is deterministic: measure the direction, magnitude, and quality of bars following the large move.

#### Distinguishing observable

The mechanism is distinguished from MECH-F02 (shock-induced cascade) by measuring the ASYMMETRY of the post-move path, not the continuation itself. F02 assumes all large moves produce continuation (forced adjustments). X04 distinguishes between informative moves (continuation) and positioning moves (reversal) based on the post-move path quality.

The mechanism is distinguished from post-magnitude directional drift (exhausted) by measuring the QUALITY of the post-move path (direction, magnitude, bar quality), not just the continuation direction. Drift assumes all large moves continue; X04 distinguishes based on path asymmetry.

#### State/event transition

The transition is from "move occurred" to "move classified as informative or positioning." This occurs during the M-bar post-move window. After the window, the classification is complete and the decision is made.

#### Economic pathway

Information absorption (asymmetric continuation) → move is genuine → continuation likely → follow the move direction.
Positioning reversal (symmetric retracement) → move is temporary → reversal likely → fade the move direction.

#### Falsification

The mechanism is falsified if:
- Post-move path asymmetry does not predict whether the move continues or reverses
- Information-driven and positioning-driven moves show identical subsequent economics
- The asymmetry measure is random relative to subsequent outcomes

#### Data requirements

- M1 OHLCV for a single instrument (e.g., USATECHIDXUSD)
- S (threshold for large move detection) — frozen at registration
- M (post-move observation window) — frozen at registration
- Asymmetry metric definition — frozen at registration

#### Execution feasibility

The information is observable in real time. After a large move is detected, the post-move path asymmetry can be measured as subsequent bars complete. Entry after classification, exit at session close.

#### Nearest prior mechanisms

- MECH-F02 (shock-induced cascade): F02 assumes all large moves produce continuation. X04 distinguishes between informative and positioning moves based on post-move path. Different mechanism (forced adjustment vs. information classification).
- Post-magnitude directional drift (exhausted): drift assumes continuation after large moves. X04 distinguishes based on path asymmetry. Different observable (continuation vs. asymmetry).
- Mean reversion (exhausted): mean reversion assumes reversion after large moves. X04 distinguishes based on path asymmetry. Different mechanism (information vs. reversion).
