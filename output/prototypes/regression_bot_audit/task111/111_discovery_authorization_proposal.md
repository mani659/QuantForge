# TASK111 — Bounded Regression Discovery Authorization Proposal (PROPOSED, NOT AUTHORIZED)

**Status:** proposal prepared for the owner. No discovery cycle conducted,
no mechanism selected or invented, no authorization recorded. If unapproved,
nothing follows from this document.

## 1. Governing basis (why this vehicle exists)

- §99.4 prohibits a new mechanism-discovery cycle under the freeze and
  requires a genuinely new idea to be "brought to governance separately."
  This proposal IS that separate vehicle.
- §99.5 anticipates a "governance-authorized mechanism-discovery exercise"
  beginning from frozen negative-knowledge (§99.1) and the §98 library as
  non-authorization constraints, feeding the restart condition (a genuinely
  distinct mechanism, sufficiently defined for the formulation gate).
- Role §21: ChatGPT cannot self-authorize research; authorization comes
  through established governance. Ownership acts through applicable
  procedures (§21: ownership ≠ unilateral override).

## 2. Purpose

Identify a genuinely distinct regression-related economic mechanism that
could eventually support an executable trading strategy — documentation-grade
hypotheses only, as specified in §7.

## 3. Scope and fixed budget

- **One discovery cycle**, executed as a single bounded task: output cap of
  at most three fully specified causal hypotheses plus a
  discarded-alternatives log with reasons; completion of that task is the
  fixed stopping point.
- **No automatic continuation.** Whatever it finds, the cycle ends with a
  hypothesis record that still requires a §99.1 ruling and the §99.3
  formulation gate before anything else may happen.
- **Concept status:** no mechanism selected or invented by this proposal.
  Selection follows the authorized process or not at all.

## 4. Distinctness standard (binding on the cycle, if authorized)

A change of estimator, indicator, timeframe, parameter, or implementation
alone does not establish a new economic mechanism. Each candidate must pass
the repository's own mechanism-identity test (task99 §2 structure: shared
component, material distinction, same economic question, same downstream
event, same counterfactual, same participant mechanism) against every
§99.1 family and the R1/R2/EXT-054/MDP/PROTO-TREND-001 records. A candidate
that fails is logged as discarded, not re-expressed.

## 5. Binding negative knowledge

R1, R2, EXT-054, EXT-062/MDP, PROTO-TREND-001, DISC-021/022, NEG-065/066,
all §99.1 families, and Batch 1 closures remain fully binding. The cycle
may study these records as exclusion boundaries; it may not re-argue,
rescue, or re-express them.

## 6. Permitted inputs (exhaustive)

Handoff §§98–99/§§101–110 records; role contract; the regression-bot audit
trail (tasks 51/57/66/68/74/77/95–110 as cited); closure records;
bibliographic verification of cited primary sources. No other inputs.

## 7. Data and execution restrictions (absolute for this cycle)

No market-data evaluation, backtesting, simulation, parameter search,
estimation on data, new data collection, holdout access, recorder
deployment, runtime/MT5 contact, implementation, or candidate activity of
any kind. The deliverable below is writable from records and reasoning
alone; any use of market data voids the cycle.

## 8. Deliverable of the authorized cycle (not of this proposal)

A small evidence-grounded hypothesis set: per hypothesis, proposed
mechanism, falsifiable prediction, relationship to each protected
exclusion, and rationale for possible admission — plus the discarded log.
This proposal itself contains zero hypotheses.

## 9. Stopping rules

Stop with a record if: no genuinely distinct mechanism is supported; the
output cap is reached; the budget (single task) is exhausted; or admission
rules prohibit further work. Findings return to governance; no formulation,
registration, or evaluation follows without new explicit decisions.

## 10. Separation (this proposal authorizes none of the following)

Candidate registration or activation; strategy implementation; backtesting,
simulation, optimization, or economic validation; evaluation-data or holdout
access; paper trading, deployment, or runtime changes; governance or
protected-ruling changes. Later implementation/evaluation needs its own
scope, specification, budgets, data permissions, and authorization. HOLD
remains in force throughout; the cycle operates as a bounded
owner-authorized documentation exception, not a research-branch activation.
